## Regulation and safety

Bullwaves trades under the name of Equitex Capital Limited (registration no. 8434948-1), which states it is authorised and regulated by the Financial Services Authority (FSA) of Seychelles under licence SD185. On the FSA's capital markets register we found Equitex Capital Limited listed as a securities dealer with the trade name BullWaves (checked 2026-11-05); the licence number itself is not shown on that page, so SD185 is taken from the broker's own legal documents. Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than top-tier regulators, and the broker's own notice says clients fall outside EU MiFID II protections. ETX Services Limited (Cyprus) is appointed as independent representative and distributor.

## Trading conditions

The site lists a Standard and a Swap-Free account from $100, and an ECN account with spreads quoted from 0.1 (the unit is not specified on the page). Maximum leverage is 1:500 on all account types. The site shows a warning that 75.2% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs with this provider. Residents of several countries, including the United States and China, are excluded.

## Platforms

Trading runs on MetaTrader 5, with a client area mobile app and a copy trading app.

## Who is it for?

Bullwaves may suit experienced traders comfortable with offshore regulation who want high leverage on MT5. Those who prioritise strong regulatory protection should consider that the only licence found is Seychelles-based. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of losing money.
