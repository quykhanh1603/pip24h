## Regulation and safety

Versus Trade's Trust & Transparency page says the broker is incorporated in Mauritius (registration 225852) and licensed by the Mauritius Financial Services Commission under licence GB25204646. The website itself is operated by Versus Trade Ltd, registered in Saint Lucia in 2024 (number 2024-00586), and the site states it holds no Malaysian licence. The Mauritius FSC's licensee register was not accessible during our research (access was blocked on 2026-11-16), so the licence is marked unverified. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction without an investor compensation scheme, and the broker does not accept clients from the EU/EEA, UK, USA or Canada.

## Trading conditions

The minimum deposit is $10. Quoted average spreads on the Pro account include 0.7 pips on EUR/USD and 1.4 pips on gold; gold is shown from 0.6 pips on a Raw account, 1.2 on Pro and 2.0 on Standard. Leverage is listed at up to 1:2000 on forex and gold, 1:500 on indices and crypto, and 1:40 on stocks. Markets cover forex, commodities, stocks, indices and crypto CFDs, plus "Versus pairs" that track the relative performance of two assets. Deposits and withdrawals are advertised as fee-free.

## Platforms

Trading is via MetaTrader 5 on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and a web terminal, and the broker also offers a mobile app and copy trading.

## Who is it for?

Versus Trade suits experienced traders outside the EU, UK and US who want low minimums and high leverage and accept the risks of a new offshore broker.
