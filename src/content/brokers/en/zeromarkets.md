## Regulation and safety

ZERO Markets lists several entities in its footer: Zero Markets Australia Pty Ltd (ASIC, AFSL 313016), Zero Markets (NZ) Limited (FMA New Zealand, FSP 569807) and Zero Financial Ltd (Mauritius FSC, licence GB21026308). We confirmed Zero Markets (NZ) Limited on the FMA's licensed-providers page on 2026-11-07, where it appears as an active derivatives issuer licensed on 27 August 2018. The ASIC and Mauritius licences could not be confirmed on their registers during this check. The website itself is operated by Zero Markets LLC, incorporated in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, which the broker states is not regulated by an EU authority and offers no EU investor compensation.

## Trading conditions

The Standard account offers spreads from 1.0 pip with no commission, and Super ZERO offers spreads from 0.0 pips with a $2.5 commission per lot per side; both have a $100 minimum opening balance. Product pages advertise leverage up to 1:500, although the support centre describes 1:5 to 1:30 depending on client and instrument, so leverage depends on the entity serving you.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and WebTrader are available, with copy trading, social trading and PAMM options.

## Who is it for?

It suits active traders who want raw spreads on MetaTrader and are comfortable checking which entity and regulator applies to their account. Leveraged CFDs carry a high risk of loss.
