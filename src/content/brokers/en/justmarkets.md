## Regulation and safety

JustMarkets names four regulated entities on its regulations page. JustMarkets Ltd is a Cyprus Investment Firm under CySEC licence 401/21, which the CySEC register lists with a licence date of 8 June 2021 and the approved domain justmarkets.eu. The group's other entities are Just Global Markets Ltd (Seychelles FSA, Securities Dealer licence SD088), Just Global Markets (VG) Limited (British Virgin Islands FSC, SIBA/L/24/1177) and Just Global Markets (MU) Limited (Mauritius FSC, GB22200881). We did not confirm these three on their regulators' registers, so they are shown as unverified. Which entity serves a client depends on the client's country of residence.

## Trading conditions

According to the account pages, Standard and Standard Cent accounts require $10; Standard has spreads from 0.3 pips with no commission. Pro and Raw Spread accounts require $200; Pro has spreads from 0.1 pips and Raw Spread from 0 pips plus $3 per side per lot. Leverage up to 1:3000 is advertised, which is not available to EU retail clients under the Cyprus entity. One help article cites lower minimums ($1 and $100), so check current figures before depositing.

## Platforms

Accounts run on MetaTrader 4 (Standard Cent, Standard, Pro, Raw Spread) and MetaTrader 5 (Standard, Pro, Raw Spread).

## Who is it for?

Small-deposit traders who want MetaTrader access and a choice of spread-only or commission accounts. Because of the high advertised leverage on offshore entities, it suits experienced traders who manage risk carefully.

Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
