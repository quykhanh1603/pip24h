## Regulation and safety

Eightcap lists three non-UK entities on its homepage: Eightcap Global Limited (Securities Commission of The Bahamas, SIA-F220), Eightcap International Ltd (Seychelles FSA, SD100) and Eightcap International Trading (Mauritius FSC, GB25204603). The FCA firm checker shows Eightcap Group Ltd (FRN 921296) as authorised since 23 December 2020 (checked 2026-11-03). The broker's site also refers to ASIC and CySEC oversight, but we did not confirm those licences on their registers in this run. Which entity holds your account depends on your country, and protections differ by entity.

## Trading conditions

Per the broker's FAQ, Eightcap was founded in Melbourne in 2015 and requires a minimum deposit of $100 (or equivalent). The Raw account advertises spreads from 0.0 pips with a commission of $3.50 per lot per side; individual market pages show Raw minimums such as 0.2 pips on AUDUSD. Leverage depends on the entity and whether you are a retail or professional client.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, TradingView and TradeLocker are offered, with more than 850 CFD instruments.

## Who is it for?

Active traders who want raw-spread pricing and a choice of platforms, and who understand that offshore entities offer weaker investor protection than FCA-regulated ones.

CFDs are leveraged products and most retail accounts lose money.
