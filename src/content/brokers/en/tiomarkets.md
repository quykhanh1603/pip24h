## Regulation and safety

TIOmarkets states that the entity you trade with depends on your country of residence. TIO Markets UK Limited appears on the FCA register under reference 488900, authorised since 26 August 2009. Trade i.o. Ltd, the Seychelles entity (Securities Dealer licence SD167 according to the broker's About page), is named on the Seychelles FSA's capital markets list, which does not display licence numbers. Clients outside the UK are generally onboarded to the offshore entity, which carries weaker protection than the FCA regime. The FCA has also published warnings about clone firms using similar names, so clients should check they are on tiomarkets.com.

## Trading conditions

Published account specs on the broker's articles: Standard from $20 with spreads from 1.1 pips and no commission; Nano from $20 with spreads from 0.6 pips plus $6 per round-turn lot (MT5 only); Raw from $250 with spreads from 0.0 pips plus $6 commission; VIP Black from $1,000 with spreads from 0.3 pips. Leverage up to 1:500 is quoted on request for several accounts.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 are available on desktop, web and mobile, with hedging allowed.

## Who is it for?

It suits traders who want a low entry deposit and raw-spread pricing, and UK clients who want FCA oversight. International clients should note the offshore entity. CFDs carry a high risk of losing money.
