## Regulation and safety

Purple Trading is operated by L.F. Investment Limited, a Cyprus Investment Firm in Limassol. The CySEC register lists the firm under licence 271/15 (dated 6 April 2015) and shows purple-trading.com among its approved domains (checked 2026-11-14). As a CySEC firm it falls under EU investor-protection rules, including an investor compensation policy.

The broker's own risk warning states that 74.37% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs with the provider.

## Trading conditions

The website lists ECN spreads such as 0.3 for EUR/USD and GBP/USD, 0.5 for USD/CHF and GBP/JPY, 0.07 for gold, 0.6 for the DAX and 0.3 for the S&P 500, with BTC/USD at 3.29. Retail leverage follows EU limits: 1:30 on forex pairs, 1:20 on gold and indices, 1:10 on oil and 1:1 on BTC/USD. Founding year and minimum deposit are not stated on the homepage, and third-party sources disagree, so they are omitted here.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on TradingView, cTrader, MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

Purple Trading suits EEA-based traders who want a CySEC-regulated broker with ECN-style pricing and a choice of platforms. It does not accept clients from outside the EEA, and CFDs carry a high risk of loss.
