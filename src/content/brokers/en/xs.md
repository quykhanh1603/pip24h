## Regulation and safety

XS's regulations page lists eight entities: XS Prime Ltd (ASIC licence 374409), XS Markets Ltd (CySEC 412/22), XS ZA (Pty) Ltd (FSCA FSP 53199), XS Trade Services Ltd (Mauritius FSC GB25204786), XS Ltd (Seychelles FSA SD089), XS Finance Ltd (Labuan FSA MB/21/0081), XS COM Financial Consultation L.L.C (UAE Capital Market Authority 20200000339) and XS United (Kuwait). These numbers come from the broker's own page and were not matched against the regulators' registers in this review. Protection depends on which entity a client is onboarded with; ASIC and CySEC entities are subject to stricter rules than the offshore ones.

## Trading conditions

The site states the company has operated since 2010. Cent, Micro and Standard accounts show no minimum deposit and spreads from 1.1 pips with no commission; Pro starts at 0.7 pips with a $500 minimum; Elite offers spreads from 0.1 pips plus a $3 commission and also needs $500. Maximum leverage is advertised as 1:1000 on Cent and Micro and 1:2000 on the other accounts. Leverage limits differ by entity and local rules.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and the XS Trading App. The Cent account is available only on MT5.

## Who is it for?

Traders who want a low entry barrier and a choice of account types may find it suitable, but the higher leverage on offer should be treated with caution. Check which entity your account is held with before depositing. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
