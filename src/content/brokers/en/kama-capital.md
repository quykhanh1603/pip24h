## Regulation and safety

KAMA Capital operates through three entities. KAMA Capital Ltd (registration 193899 GBC) holds an Investment Dealer (Full-Service Dealer, excluding Underwriting) licence from the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius; the entity appears on the FSC's register of licensees with a 30 January 2023 date (checked 2026-11-26), while the licence number GB22200704 is taken from the broker's website. KAMA Capital Securities Broker L.L.C. is described as licensed by the UAE Capital Market Authority for Category 1 and Category 5 activities, and KAMA Capital LLC is registered with the St. Vincent & the Grenadines FSA (registration 2049, a registration rather than a licence). We could not confirm the UAE or SVG entries on a regulator register. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than tier-1 regulators.

## Trading conditions

The company was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Dubai. Four account types are listed: Classic ($20 minimum, leverage up to 1:500, no commission), Prime ($5,000, up to 1:500), Institutional ($50,000, up to 1:100) and HFT (on request, up to 1:400). Spreads are described as low, ultra-low or raw rather than as figures. Instruments are forex, metals, commodities, indices and stock CFDs.

## Platforms

Trading is on MetaTrader 5, with iOS and Android apps and FIX API access for institutional and HFT clients.

## Who is it for?

KAMA Capital may suit traders who accept an offshore licence and want a low entry deposit or institutional-style accounts. Those who prefer a long track record or top-tier oversight should compare brokers regulated by the FCA, ASIC or similar. CFDs carry a high risk of rapid loss.
