## Regulation and safety

Eplanet Brokers LTD is incorporated in Mohéli, Comoros (IBC no. HY00923038), which is a company registration rather than a financial licence. Its group company Nebula Ventures Ltd (Vanuatu company no. 700819) is listed on the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission's financial dealers licensee list with classes A, B and C and active status (checked 2026-11-13). Vanuatu is an offshore (Tier 3) regime. The site states it does not serve EU residents or residents of sanctioned or FATF-listed jurisdictions.

## Trading conditions

Four accounts are published: Zero ($30 minimum, spread from 9 points), ECN ($300, from 6 points), Gold ($500, from 0 points) and Elite ($3,000, from 0 points). Maximum leverage is listed as 500 on all accounts and conditions may vary by region.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 5 and cTrader.
