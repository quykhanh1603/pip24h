Bybit is a major cryptocurrency exchange offering spot trading, derivatives, and copy trading, known for deep liquidity on its futures products.

## Trading Fees

Bybit uses a maker/taker fee model, typically around 0.01% for makers and 0.06% for takers on spot trading, with separate fee tiers for futures contracts.

## Should you trade with Bybit?

Suited to traders who specifically want crypto spot and derivatives trading with deep liquidity — note this is a crypto exchange, not a traditionally regulated forex broker, so the risk profile and oversight differ accordingly.
