## Regulation and safety

Radex Markets operates through RADEX MARKETS Ltd Co (company number 810629-1), which the broker says holds Securities Dealer Licence SD226 from the Seychelles Financial Services Authority. The FSA's capital markets entity list names RADEX MARKETS LTD at a Victoria, Mahe address; that public list does not display licence numbers, so SD226 itself is taken from the broker's website. Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than the UK, Australia or EU. Finance Magnates reported in 2021 that the brand was launched by the operator of GO Markets.

## Trading conditions

The Standard account carries no commission, while the Raw account charges $2.50 per side. Both require a $100 minimum deposit and allow leverage up to 1:500. The site advertises spreads from zero pips, with sample quotes such as 0.00 on EUR/USD and 0.20 on GBP/USD, though it notes these are indicative. More than 350 products are offered across forex, metals, index CFDs and crypto CFDs.

## Platforms

Trading is available on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

It suits cost-conscious traders comfortable with offshore regulation who want MetaTrader access and a moderate minimum deposit. Traders needing top-tier regulatory protection should look elsewhere. CFDs carry a high risk of losing money.
