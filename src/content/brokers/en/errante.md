## Regulation and safety

Errante states that Errante Securities (Seychelles) Ltd holds Seychelles Financial Services Authority licence SD038, and that a separate entity, Notely Trading Limited (errante.eu), holds CySEC licence 383/20 in Cyprus. The Seychelles FSA's capital markets list shows Errante Securities (Seychelles) Limited, but we could not confirm licence number SD038 or the CySEC entry on the regulators' registers, so both are shown as unverified. Note that the FSA Seychelles has also published an alert about another firm citing the same SD038 number, so check the register directly.

Japan's Kanto Local Finance Bureau lists Errante Securities (Seychelles) Ltd among firms soliciting derivatives business without registration (notice dated 28 August 2025, checked 2026-11-21), and the IOSCO I-SCAN database carries a related alert. The firm does not accept clients from several jurisdictions, including the USA and Canada.

## Trading conditions

The Standard account starts from a $50 deposit. The Tailor Made account advertises spreads from 0.0, and the VIP tier offers tight spreads without a published figure. Crypto CFDs are described as having high leverage, but no general leverage figure is given on the homepage. The company says it started in 2019.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, cTrader and TradingView are offered.

## Who is it for?

Platform variety may appeal to active traders, but the offshore licence and the Japanese notice mean clients should check their local rules and the regulators' registers first.

Trading leveraged products involves a high risk of loss.
