FXCC is an ECN-model forex and CFD broker founded in 2010 and headquartered in Cyprus, regulated by both CySEC (Europe) and MISA (Mwali International Services Authority).

## Trading Costs

FXCC mainly offers a single account type — ECN XL — with spreads from 0.4 pips (averaging around 0.6 pips on EUR/USD) and no added trading commission. A swap-free Islamic account is also available for traders who require it.

## Should You Trade With FXCC?

FXCC suits traders who prioritize low trading costs and don't want to commit a large amount of starting capital, thanks to its no-minimum-deposit policy. That said, the broker only supports the MetaTrader platforms and offers limited educational resources, making it a better fit for experienced traders with their own strategy than for beginners who need more guidance.
