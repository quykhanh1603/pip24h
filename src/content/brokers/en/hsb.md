## Regulation and safety

HSB Investasi is the trading name of PT Handal Semesta Berjangka, a Jakarta-based futures broker that says it started operating in 2018. Its legal page lists BAPPEBTI (Indonesia's commodity futures trading regulator) licence numbers including 001/BAPPEBTI/SI/05/2018, together with Bank Indonesia and OJK references and memberships of the ICDX exchange and the Indonesia Clearing House. The broker also states that client money sits in segregated accounts at banks approved by BAPPEBTI. These details come from the broker's own site; Pip24h could not retrieve the regulator's register to confirm them, so the licences are marked unverified.

## Trading conditions

The site offers three account types. The Micro account needs Rp350,000 and offers spreads from 0.01 pips, a $3 per lot commission and leverage of 1:200 to 1:400. The Standard account needs Rp2.8 million and the Premium account Rp70 million, with a lower $1 per lot commission and leverage of 1:200. Spreads are floating and the site publishes average spreads per instrument. The broker lists more than 45 instruments across forex, gold and oil, indices and single stocks, and advertises a client-fund insurance programme of up to USD 1 million.

## Platforms

Trading is available on MetaTrader 5, the HSB Trading app (Android and iOS) and a web terminal; deposits and withdrawals are handled inside the app.

## Who is it for?

HSB suits Indonesian residents who want a locally registered futures broker with a small starting deposit and an app-based workflow. International traders should note the service is built around Indonesian regulation, and as with any leveraged product, losses can exceed expectations.
