## Regulation and safety

The website footer lists several PU Prime entities. PU Prime Limited appears as a Securities Dealer on the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles capital markets register, though the register page does not show a licence number or status. The footer also cites a Mauritius FSC licence (GB23202672) for PU Prime Ltd, which we could not locate on the FSC Mauritius register, so it is shown as unverified. Both are offshore jurisdictions.

The UK Financial Conduct Authority lists "PU prime / Pacific Union (Seychelles) Limited" and the puprime.com website on its warning list, stating the firm is not authorised by the FCA (first published 10 May 2023, last updated 20 April 2026, checked 2026-10-30). The FCA says clients dealing with such firms would not have access to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the FSCS.

## Trading conditions

According to PU Prime's account pages, the Standard account has spreads from 1.3 pips with no commission and needs $50 to trade; Cent starts at $20; Prime offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus $3.50 per side per lot (from $1,000); ECN offers 0.0 pips plus $1 per side per lot (from $10,000). Marketing pages advertise leverage up to 1:1000, while the help centre gives 1:500 as the default with options between 1:100 and 1:1000, subject to product limits.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, the PU Prime app, PU Web Trader and PU Copy Trading.

## Who is it for?

Traders outside restricted jurisdictions who accept offshore regulation. Anyone in the UK should note the FCA warning above.

Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
