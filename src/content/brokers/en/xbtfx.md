## Regulation and safety

XBTFX's legal documents page states that accounts are opened with XBTFX LLC, incorporated in Antigua and Barbuda (registration ILLC001), and that the company "is not required to hold any financial services license or authorization" there. No licence from a financial regulator was found, so client protection depends on the company's own policies rather than regulator rules or a compensation scheme. The site says it has operated since 2019 and describes a client-funds and safety policy on a separate page. It does not serve residents of the USA, Quebec, Belgium, Belarus, Russia, North Korea, Iran or Sudan.

## Trading conditions

Two account types share the same execution environment. The Pro account shows variable spreads from 0.0 pips plus $2.75 per $100k traded on FX, and a 0.05% commission on crypto; the Standard account has spreads from 1 pip with no separate commission. Minimum deposit is $10. The site advertises dynamic leverage up to 1000:1 depending on the instrument, more than 400 instruments across six asset classes and 24/7 crypto trading. Share CFD costs are 0.3% for US and 0.45% for EU and Asian stocks.

## Platforms

Trading is available on MetaTrader 5 and on XBTFX's own Mega1 platform (desktop, web and mobile), with PAMM, MAM and copy-trading services and a trading API.

## Who is it for?

XBTFX suits experienced traders who want crypto and forex CFDs with low minimum funding and accept that the operator is unregulated. Traders who prioritise regulatory protection should consider licensed alternatives.
