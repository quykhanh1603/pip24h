## Regulation and safety

Key to Markets International Limited (Ebene, Mauritius, company number 169425) states that it is regulated by the Mauritius Financial Services Commission as an Investment Dealer, licence GB19024503. The FSC register lists the firm with licence type SEC-2.1B (Investment Dealer, full service dealer excluding underwriting) dated 17 December 2019 (checked 2026-11-17); the number itself is taken from the broker's site. K2M Ltd of Cyprus acts as its payment processing company and independent representative. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction, so investor protection is lighter than under the FCA, ASIC or CySEC.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises ECN spreads from 0.0 pips and Cent, Standard and Pro trading accounts, plus copy trading and PAMM. Minimum deposit and maximum leverage were not stated on the pages reviewed, so confirm them before funding an account.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and an MT5 web terminal are offered.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders comfortable with an offshore-regulated broker and interested in copy trading or PAMM may consider it. Services are not offered to residents of the USA, Iran or North Korea and are not intended for UK or EU residents. CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
