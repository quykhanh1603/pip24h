## Regulation and safety

GO Markets Pty Ltd holds Australian Financial Services Licence 254963. The ASIC professional register lists the licence as current (checked 2026-11-12). The licence was granted in 2004 and covers dealing in foreign exchange contracts and derivatives for retail and wholesale clients. Disputes are handled via the Australian Financial Complaints Authority.

The group's website also names GO Markets Ltd (CySEC licence 322/17) for EU clients and GO Markets International Ltd (Seychelles FSA securities dealer licence SD043). We did not confirm these two on their regulators' registers, so they are marked unverified. Which entity you contract with depends on your country of residence, and protections differ between them.

## Trading conditions

GO Markets says it launched in Australia in 2006. Its account-type page lists a Micro account with a $10 minimum deposit, and the EU pages state there is no minimum deposit requirement. Leverage is capped at 1:30 for Australian retail clients, while the site states up to 1:500 for wholesale clients. Exact spreads and commissions should be checked on the live account page.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and cTrader, with copy-trading and PAMM options mentioned on the company site.

## Who is it for?

Traders who want an ASIC-regulated broker with a long track record and a low entry deposit. Traders outside Australia should confirm which GO Markets entity will hold their account. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of losing money.
