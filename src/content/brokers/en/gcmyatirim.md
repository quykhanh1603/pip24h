## Regulation and safety

The GCM Yatırım website names GCM Yatırım Menkul Değerler A.Ş. as an investment firm authorised by Turkey's Capital Markets Board (SPK) as a "geniş yetkili" (broad-authority) firm under number G-039 (398). Turkey's public disclosure platform, KAP, lists the same authorisation dated 26 January 2016. We did not find the firm on an official SPK register page during this run, so the licence is marked unverified. The company is based in Maslak, Istanbul, and says it was founded in 2012.

## Trading conditions

GCM says it earns from the bid/ask spread and applies no additional cost to forex trades, with swap rates published per instrument. Under SPK rules, leverage for Turkish clients is capped at 1:10. The site states no clear minimum deposit; its pages mention 50,000 TL or the foreign-currency equivalent when moving from a demo to a live account.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on GCM Trader, MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, GCM Borsa Trader (stocks) and Matriks Trader, covering forex, VİOP, stocks and options.

## Who is it for?

GCM Yatırım suits Turkish-resident traders who want a locally authorised broker with both forex and local market access. Traders seeking high leverage or English-language support may prefer other options. Forex and CFD trading carries a high risk of loss.
