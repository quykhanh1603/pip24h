## Regulation and safety

The Australian TMGM site names Trademax Australia Limited (ABN 76 162 331 311) as the operating company and states AFS licence (AFSL) 436416 issued by ASIC. We could not open ASIC's register during this review, so the licence number is marked unverified; ASIC's own media release does identify Trademax Australia Limited as an Australian CFD and margin FX provider. In 2024 ASIC issued interim stop orders under the design and distribution obligations that stopped it opening new retail accounts; ASIC states the orders were lifted from 25 June 2024. The site says client funds are held with National Australia Bank and that professional indemnity insurance of up to AUD 10 million is in place.

## Trading conditions

The minimum deposit is $100, with spreads advertised from 0.0 pips. Maximum leverage shown on the Australian site is 1:30, in line with ASIC's retail limits; other TMGM entities may offer different terms.

## Platforms

TMGM offers MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 on desktop, web, Android and iPhone.

## Who is it for?

TMGM suits traders who want an Australian-regulated provider with the standard MetaTrader platforms and low spreads. Those seeking high leverage will find the Australian site restrictive. CFD trading involves a high risk of losing money.
