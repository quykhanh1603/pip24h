Vantage Markets (formerly Vantage FX) combines competitive ECN pricing with an accessible minimum deposit, making it approachable for newer traders while still offering the raw spreads experienced traders look for.

## Trading Fees

The RAW account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus commission, while the Standard STP account bundles costs into the spread with no separate commission — a simpler option for beginners.

## Should you trade with Vantage Markets?

A good fit for traders who want ECN-level pricing without a high deposit barrier, and for those interested in following other traders through copy trading.
