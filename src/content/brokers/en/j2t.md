## Regulation and safety

J2T Services Ltd (registration HE 341520, Limassol, Cyprus) operates the Just2Trade brand. It is licensed by the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC) as a Cypriot investment firm under licence number 281/15, issued on 25 September 2015. We confirmed the entry on CySEC's list of Cypriot investment firms on 2026-11-09, where it appears as "J2T Services Ltd (ex Lime Trading (CY) Ltd)". The firm states that it is a member of the Cyprus Investor Compensation Fund.

The website states that services are provided only to Professional Clients and Eligible Counterparties. It also notes that Lime Trading (CY) Ltd has ceased providing services to the UK market.

## Trading conditions

J2T says it has provided brokerage services since 2016. Its forex page advertises spreads from 0.0 pips; the bond page mentions leverage of up to 1:3. A minimum deposit and a headline maximum leverage are not stated on the pages we reviewed, so check the client terms before opening an account.

## Platforms

The broker lists MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 for forex and CFDs, CQG for global futures and Sterling Trader Pro for equities, with demo access on Windows, web, Android and iOS.

## Who is it for?

Because J2T serves professional clients and eligible counterparties only, it is aimed at experienced traders who want access to futures, stocks and forex through institutional-style platforms rather than at beginners. Trading involves risk; you can lose more than you deposit with leveraged products.
