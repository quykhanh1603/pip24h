## Regulation and safety

CySEC's register lists IQBroker Europe Ltd (previously IQ Option Europe Ltd) under licence 247/14, dated 30 July 2014; IQ Option says this licence serves EEA residents only and that Investor Compensation Fund cover applies only to EEA clients. Wikipedia also reports a Bahamas licence for the Quadcode Markets entity, which we did not check. It further records regulator warnings and alert-list entries in other jurisdictions (including ASIC in 2016, MAS since 2017 and India's RBI), plus CySEC fines in 2016 and a €450,000 settlement in 2019. We did not check these lists directly on this run, so verify the entity and your local regulator's current position before depositing.

## Trading conditions

IQ Option's pages state a $10 minimum deposit and positions from $1. Spreads are the main cost; for example, index spreads are listed from 0.4 on US500. Advertised leverage varies by asset and region, with the broker's own pages quoting up to 20x on stock and ETF CFDs and far higher on commodities, indices and some forex pairs outside regulated regions.

## Platforms

Trading is on IQ Option's own web platform and apps for iOS and Android, with a free demo account. MetaTrader is not offered.

## Who is it for?

It suits small-balance traders who like a simple mobile-first interface. Those who prioritise strong regulation should confirm which entity they would use. CFDs carry a high risk of rapid loss.
