OKX is a major global crypto exchange offering a broad product suite that spans spot trading, derivatives, and an integrated Web3 wallet.

## Trading Fees

OKX uses a maker/taker fee model, with rates that scale down based on 30-day trading volume and the amount of the platform's native token held.

## Should you trade with OKX?

A good fit for traders who want a full-featured crypto platform beyond simple spot trading, including derivatives and Web3 access — again, keep in mind this operates under crypto-exchange licensing, not traditional forex broker regulation.
