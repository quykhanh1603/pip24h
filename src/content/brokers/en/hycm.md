HYCM (Henyep Capital Markets) is a UK-based broker with one of the longest operating histories in the industry, part of a group founded in 1977.

## Trading Fees

HYCM offers a choice between fixed and floating spread accounts, both charging through the spread with no separate commission on standard trading.

## Should you trade with HYCM?

A solid option for traders who value a long track record and multi-jurisdiction regulation, and who don't need the absolute tightest raw spreads.
