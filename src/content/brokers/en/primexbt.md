## Regulation and safety

PrimeXBT's website names PrimeXBT Trading Services Ltd, registered in Castries, Saint Lucia, as its legal entity and lists several authorisations: South Africa's FSCA (45697), a Seychelles FSA broker-dealer licence (SD162), two El Salvador digital-asset registrations and a Saint Lucia incorporation number. We could not confirm any of these on the regulators' own registers, so all are marked unverified. Which protections apply depends on the entity you are onboarded with.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises leverage from 1x to 1000x, no minimum deposit, and spreads "from 0" on EUR/USD. These are marketing figures and actual costs vary by instrument and market conditions. A free demo account with a $10,000 virtual balance is offered.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on PXTrader 2.0, PrimeXBT's own platform for crypto futures and CFDs, or on MetaTrader 5. The company also offers a wallet and says assets are secured with Fireblocks.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders who want crypto futures and CFDs in one account and accept offshore regulation. High leverage makes it unsuitable for beginners.

Trading involves risk and you can lose all of your capital.
