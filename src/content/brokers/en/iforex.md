## Regulation and safety

iFOREX is the brand of Formula Investment House Ltd, which is regulated by the British Virgin Islands Financial Services Commission according to the broker's website. We could not check that licence on the BVI register. European clients are served by ICFD Ltd, trading as iFOREX Europe. This entity appears on the CySEC register of Cypriot investment firms under licence 143/11, dated 23 May 2011 (checked 2026-11-04).

## Trading conditions

The website states maximum leverage of up to 1:400 and advertises zero commissions, directing visitors to a pricing page for spreads. No minimum deposit figure is given on the homepage. Leverage for retail clients under the CySEC entity is capped by European rules and is lower than the headline figure.

## Platforms

Trading is offered through the proprietary iFOREX app, with tools such as live rates, an economic calendar and trading signals. MetaTrader is not listed on the homepage.

## Who is it for?

The broker describes itself as active since 1996, which makes it one of the longer-established CFD providers. It may suit traders who prefer an all-in-one proprietary app, but anyone who needs MetaTrader or published spread tables should check the current trading conditions first. CFDs are complex instruments and carry a high risk of losing money.
