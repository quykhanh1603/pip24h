## Regulation and safety

IG Markets Limited appears on the FCA's investment firms register under reference 195355. IG's own comparison page says its UK entities are FCA-authorised, with FSCS protection up to £85,000, and that IG Europe GmbH is authorised by BaFin and the Deutsche Bundesbank. It also names ASIC, FINMA, FSCA, MAS, FMA, DFSA, JFSA, CFTC/NFA and BMA oversight for other regions; we did not check those on their registers. The parent, IG Group, has been listed on the London Stock Exchange since 2000.

## Trading conditions

Minimum deposits depend on the entity: £250 in the UK, €300 in Europe, $450 in Australia and Singapore, and $50 by card for IG International. We did not find a spread table on the pages we read, so check IG's current pricing and leverage limits for your region.

## Platforms

Clients can use IG's own web and mobile platforms. UK clients can also connect third-party platforms including TradingView, MetaTrader 4 and 5, and ProRealTime.

## Who is it for?

IG suits traders who prioritise a long-established, listed and widely regulated provider and who can meet the higher deposit levels in some regions. CFDs are complex instruments and you can lose money rapidly due to leverage.
