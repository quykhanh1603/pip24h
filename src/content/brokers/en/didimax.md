## Regulation and safety

Didimax is the trading name of PT Didi Max Berjangka, an Indonesian futures broker based in Bandung. Its licences page lists BAPPEBTI licence 44/BAPPEBTI/SI/XII/2000, exchange membership of the Jakarta Futures Exchange (JFX), clearing membership of the Indonesian Futures Clearing House (KBI) and membership of the broker association ASPEBTINDO. We have not confirmed these entries on the BAPPEBTI register itself, so they are shown as unverified; check the current status with BAPPEBTI before depositing.

## Trading conditions

Didimax offers a single trading account. According to its account page the minimum deposit is US$50, leverage is up to 1:400 and positions can be opened from 0.01 lot. Spreads are listed from 0.9 pips in the specification table, while the instruments section says variable spreads start from 1.0 pips; no commission is mentioned. The company says it has operated since 1999.

## Platforms

Clients trade on MetaTrader 5 (Windows, macOS, Android, iOS and web) or on the Didimax Trader mobile app.

## Who is it for?

The broker mainly suits Indonesian residents who want a locally licensed futures broker with a low entry deposit and a simple account structure. Traders wanting raw-spread or commission accounts, or the protection of a tier-1 regulator, may prefer other options.

Trading leveraged products involves a high risk of loss.
