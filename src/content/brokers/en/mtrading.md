## Regulation and safety

MTrading's website says clients are served by ServiceComsvg LLC, registered in Kingstown, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (registration 1593 LLC 2021). The site does not display a licence from a financial regulator, and we found none. Instead it highlights membership of the Financial Commission, an external dispute-resolution body whose compensation fund covers up to €20,000 per claim. Finance Magnates has reported that MTrading joined the Commission, left, and later rejoined; we could not confirm the current status on the Commission's own list, and membership is not a substitute for regulation by a financial authority. The brand states it has operated since 2012.

## Trading conditions

The M.Premium account needs a $10 minimum deposit and shows spreads from 1 pip with no commission, market execution, a 20% stop-out and floating leverage up to 1:8888. The M.Pro account is marketed as zero-spread. Margin tables reduce leverage as position size grows, for example 1:8888 on small FX majors positions down to 1:10 on very large ones. Instruments include 28 currency pairs, metals, energies, indices, over 70 stock CFDs and synthetic indices. Promotions, including deposit and floating bonuses, are central to the marketing.

## Platforms

Clients trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and a web terminal, with mobile apps and Expert Advisor support.

## Who is it for?

MTrading is aimed at experienced, risk-tolerant traders outside heavily regulated jurisdictions who accept offshore counterparty risk. Beginners should be cautious about extreme leverage and bonus conditions.
