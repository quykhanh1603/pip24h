## Regulation and safety

Markets.com's site names two licensed entities. Safecap Investments Limited is authorised by CySEC under licence 092/08; we confirmed this entry on the CySEC register on 2026-11-07, where it shows a licence date of 28 July 2008 and the trade name "SCAPFX MARKETS.COM". Markets South Africa (Pty) Ltd is stated to hold FSCA licence 46860, but we could not confirm it on the FSCA search during this check. CySEC's website also lists a board decision imposing an administrative fine on Safecap; amounts and dates should be checked at the source.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises EUR/USD spreads from 0.6 pips, and its Classic account lists spreads from 0.7 pips with no commission. Minimum deposit is published as roughly $100 depending on country, though other help pages show higher figures. Leverage of up to 1:1000 is mentioned on selected instruments for non-EU entities; EU clients are subject to MiFID limits. Account tiers are tied to a "marketsClub" scheme based on deposit size.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, the Markets web terminal and the mobile app.

## Who is it for?

It suits traders who want a long-established CFD provider with a CySEC-regulated entity and a choice of MetaTrader or proprietary platforms. Check which entity serves your country, as protections and conditions differ. CFDs are high-risk products and most retail accounts lose money.
