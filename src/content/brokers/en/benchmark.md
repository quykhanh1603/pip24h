## Regulation and safety

BenchMark Finance AD is a Bulgarian investment firm supervised by the Financial Supervision Commission (FSC). Its website states authorisation number 03-0212 and says the company belongs to the BenchMark Group, founded in 2003. The firm appears on the FSC's published list of investment firms (checked 2026-11-28), and the FSC's notification register shows it has notified the intention to provide services in Poland, Germany and Italy under the freedom to provide services. The site's risk warning says 55% of retail CFD accounts lose money with this provider.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises a minimum EUR/USD spread of 0.6 pips, no fee for deposits or withdrawals, and a minimum margin of 0.25%. Instruments include currency pairs, shares, commodities, indices and cryptocurrencies, depending on the platform. The broker also provides access to the Bulgarian Stock Exchange for shares and bonds. As an EU-regulated firm it is subject to ESMA's leverage limits for retail clients, so the headline margin figure may apply only to certain client categories. Minimum deposit and maximum leverage figures were not found on the pages reviewed and are omitted.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and the proprietary BG Trader platform, with mobile apps and a demo account available. The site also publishes analysis, calendars and educational material, mostly in Bulgarian.

## Who is it for?

BenchMark Finance suits traders based in Bulgaria or elsewhere in the EU who want a locally supervised broker and the option to trade Bulgarian-listed securities alongside CFDs. Traders who need extensive English-language support or very high leverage may prefer other providers.
