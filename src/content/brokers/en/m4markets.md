## Regulation and safety

M4Markets is a co-brand shared by several group entities. Harindale Ltd holds CySEC licence 301/16 (an EU investment firm, trade name M4Markets, on the CySEC register since 27 April 2016). Oryx Finance Ltd is listed on the DFSA public register under reference F007051, authorised for dealing and arranging deals in futures with retail clients. Trinota Markets (Global) Limited is listed by the Seychelles FSA as a securities dealer (licence SD035 per the broker). A Seychelles licence is considered offshore and offers less investor protection than the CySEC or DFSA entities. Pip24h checked these registers on 2026-11-17.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises a minimum deposit of $5, raw spreads from 0.0 pips and leverage of up to 1:5000. Maximum leverage depends on the entity and client jurisdiction; EU-regulated clients cannot access the highest ratios. Check the commission schedule for raw-spread accounts before comparing costs.

## Platforms

Trading is offered on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5, with mobile, desktop and web versions.

## Who is it for?

The low entry deposit suits beginners testing a live account. Traders should confirm which regulated entity they would contract with and be aware that CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
