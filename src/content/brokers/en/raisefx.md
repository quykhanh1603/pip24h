## Regulation and safety

RaiseFX's website states that Raise Global SA (Pty) Ltd (company number 2018/616118/07) is an authorised Financial Service Provider regulated by South Africa's Financial Sector Conduct Authority under licence 50506, with offices in Sandton, Johannesburg. The legal documents page includes FSCA-related policies such as a FAIS upfront disclosure. Pip24h could not open the FSCA's register during this research on 2026-11-16, so the licence is marked unverified; a secondary data site also reports the same number and an October 2020 start date but is not a primary source. The site excludes residents of the EU, USA, Singapore, Australia, North Korea and certain other regions.

## Trading conditions

RaiseFX's about page dates its founding to November 2021, with its first client in April 2022 and a move from MT4 to MT5 in February 2023. The site shows a €100 minimum deposit, leverage up to 1:500, zero deposit fees, 24/5 support and more than 500 tradable assets: forex, indices, commodities, shares and crypto CFDs. It lists copy trading, launched in 2023. Live spread tables are on the website but average figures were not available in static content, so no spread figure is quoted here.

## Platforms

All trading is on MetaTrader 5 (desktop, mobile and tablet). The broker says its pricing comes from a "dark liquidity pool".

## Who is it for?

RaiseFX is for traders outside excluded regions who prefer an FSCA-authorised entity and MT5, and who can start with €100. The licence should be confirmed on the FSCA register before depositing.
