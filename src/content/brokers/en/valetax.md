## Regulation and safety

Valetax's footer lists three companies. Valetax International Limited (Mauritius, registration C180644) is said to hold Investment Dealer licence GB21026312 from the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius. Valetax Global Limited (St. Vincent and the Grenadines, No. 23398) is shown with no named regulator, and Valetax Processing Ltd (Cyprus) is a payment agent. We could not locate the Mauritius licence on the regulator's register, so it is marked unverified. The site notes that the entity serving you depends on onboarding, and that only that entity's documents are provided afterwards.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises a minimum deposit of $8, maximum leverage of 1:2000 and spreads from 0.0 pips. By account, it lists 1.2 pips (Cent and Standard), 0.0 (ECN), 2.0 (Booster and Bonus) and 0.6 (PRO).

## Platforms

MT4 and MT5 are available as desktop, web and mobile versions, along with a Valetax mobile app.

## Who is it for?

Small-budget traders comfortable with offshore regulation. Extremely high leverage and limited investor protection mean strict risk management is essential.

Trading leveraged products involves a high risk of loss.
