## Regulation and safety

D Prime Vanuatu Limited (company number 700238) is listed as an active licensee, classes A, B and C, on the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission's Financial Dealers Licensee List (licence date 17 April 2023, checked 2026-11-02). Vanuatu is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than top-tier regulators. The broker's licences page also cites a Seychelles Financial Services Authority licence (SD090), which we have not verified on the FSA register, and its recent client agreements name only the Vanuatu entity as contracting party. Older agreements referenced Seychelles and Mauritius entities. The company says it has over ten years of presence in the industry but does not state a founding year.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises spreads starting at 0.0 pips, leverage of up to 1:1000 and more than 10,000 instruments. Minimum deposit and commission levels were not stated on the pages we read, so check the current account terms before funding. Leverage and available products depend on the client's country and entity.

## Platforms

Doo Prime offers MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 for desktop and a mobile app on Google Play.

## Who is it for?

The broker may appeal to experienced traders who want tight spreads and high leverage and accept offshore-regulation risk. Beginners and those who prioritise strong investor protection should be cautious: high leverage magnifies losses, and most retail CFD accounts lose money.
