## Regulation and safety

BtcDana is operated by Dana Global Limited, registered in Mauritius (company no. 190203). The company states it holds Financial Services Commission (Mauritius) licence GB22200578 as a full-service Investment Dealer excluding underwriting. A search of the FSC register of licensees indicated an entry for Dana Global Limited dated 05/09/2022 in a matching category, but we could not read the register page directly to confirm the licence number, so the licence is shown as unverified. A UAE affiliate holds a CMA Category 5 licence limited to introducing and promotion; it does not execute trades or hold client funds. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction with weaker protections than FCA or ASIC.

## Trading conditions

The site advertises raw spreads from 0.0 pips, a commission-free Premium account, and leverage up to 1:500, with no hidden fees. The minimum deposit is not stated on the homepage. Residents of the USA, UK, Canada and several other countries are excluded.

## Platforms

Trading is offered on MetaTrader 5 for Windows, macOS, web, iOS and Android.
