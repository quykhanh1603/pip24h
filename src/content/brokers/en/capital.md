## Regulation and safety

Capital Com SV Investments Limited holds CySEC licence 319/17 (dated 7 April 2017), which we confirmed on the CySEC register. Capital.com's help centre also lists Capital Com (UK) Limited under FCA reference 793714 and Capital Com Australia Pty Ltd under ASIC AFSL 513393; we did not check these on their registers. The international website names Capital Com Online Investments Ltd, authorised by the Securities Commission of The Bahamas (SIA-F245), as the contracting entity. CySEC imposed a €10,000 fine on the Cyprus entity in March 2022 over market abuse breaches.

## Trading conditions

Capital.com offers more than 5,500 CFD markets covering shares, forex, indices, commodities and crypto. It earns through spreads and says it charges no deposit or withdrawal fees. The help centre gives a minimum deposit of 20 EUR/USD/GBP for most payment methods and 50 EUR for wire transfers. Leverage varies by entity and region; we saw conflicting figures, so check the limits for your jurisdiction.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on the Capital.com web terminal and mobile apps, or via MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and TradingView, with API access also listed.

## Who is it for?

It suits beginners wanting a low entry deposit and a simple proprietary platform, and traders who prefer MT4, MT5 or TradingView. Check which entity you would sign with, since protections differ. CFDs carry a high risk of rapid losses.
