HFM, formerly known as HotForex, is a multi-regulated broker offering a wide spread of account types, from a no-minimum-deposit entry account up to a raw-spread Premium account.

## Trading Fees

Entry-level accounts charge through the spread with no commission, while the Premium account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus a commission for cost-conscious active traders.

## Should you trade with HFM?

A flexible option for traders at any experience level — from complete beginners who want to start with no deposit, to more advanced traders interested in social/copy trading via HFcopy.
