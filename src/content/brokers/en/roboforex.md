RoboForex is an offshore-regulated broker known for a wide range of account types, low deposit requirements, and access to tight ECN-style pricing.

## Trading Fees

The Prime and ECN accounts offer spreads from 0.0 pips plus a commission, while the Pro-Standard account bundles costs into a slightly wider spread with no separate commission.

## Should you trade with RoboForex?

Worth considering for traders comfortable with offshore-only regulation who want low-cost entry and flexible account options, including access to stock CFDs through R StocksTrader.
