## Trading Fees

The Raw account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus a per-lot commission, while the Standard account bundles the cost into a slightly wider spread with no commission — useful for traders who prefer simpler fee structures.

## Should you trade with FP Markets?

A solid choice for traders who want ECN-level pricing from a long-established, multi-regulated broker, especially those who also want access to share CFDs via IRESS.
