## Regulation and safety

Moneta Markets operates through several legal entities. Moneta Markets Capital Ltd (FCA reference 613381) serves UK clients and appears as authorised in FCA register data (checked 2026-11-12). The broker's own legal page states that its international entities are not FCA-regulated and that FCA protections such as the FSCS, the Financial Ombudsman and FCA leverage limits do not apply to them.

The site also lists Moneta Markets (Pty) Ltd (South Africa, FSCA licence 47490), Moneta Markets Trading Limited (Mauritius, FSC GB24203391) and a Saint Lucia company registration. We could not confirm the FSCA or Mauritius entries on the regulators' registers, so they are marked unverified. A UAE entity is described as licensed for introduction and promotion only, not brokerage.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises raw (PRIME ECN) spreads from 0.0 pips and leverage up to 1:1000 on MT4 and its PRO Trader platform. The company says it has more than ten years of experience but does not publish an exact founding date. Minimum deposit figures differ between third-party sources, so check the live account pages.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, PRO Trader 2.0, a mobile AppTrader, a CopyTrader app and web versions of MT4 are listed.

## Who is it for?

UK residents deal with the FCA-authorised entity. Clients elsewhere are served by offshore entities with lighter protections, so check which entity your account is with. High leverage CFD trading carries a high risk of loss.
