## Regulation and safety

Traze trades under the Zeal Group. The Seychelles FSA's capital markets list names Zeal Capital Market (Seychelles) Limited with the trade name TRAZE (checked 2026-11-12). We could not match the licence number SD027 shown on the Traze site on that list, so no number is given here. The site also names Traze (Pty) Ltd (South Africa, FSCA FSP 48248, acting as an intermediary) and Traze Securities L.L.C (UAE, two Capital Market Authority licences). We could not confirm these on the regulators' registers, so they are marked unverified. The Seychelles entity does not serve EU, US or Canadian residents.

## Trading conditions

Per the site: Standard from $50, Cent from $15 and ECN from $200, all with leverage up to 1:2000. The minimum spread is 1.3 on Standard and Cent and 0.2 on ECN (commission may apply); units are not stated. Wikipedia gives 2023 as the launch year.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 (desktop, Mac and mobile) and the Traze mobile app.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders who accept offshore regulation and want small account sizes. Extreme leverage can wipe out a deposit quickly. CFDs carry a high risk of loss.
