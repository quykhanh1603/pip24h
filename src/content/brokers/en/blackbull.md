## Regulation and safety

BlackBull's compliance page names two entities: Black Bull Group Limited, a New Zealand company registered on the Financial Services Providers Register (FSP403326) and a participant in the Financial Services Complaints Ltd dispute scheme, and BBG Limited, a Seychelles company authorised by the Seychelles FSA under licence SD045. The FSPR registration is not a licence to supervise trading, and the Seychelles FSA is an offshore regulator. These details come from the broker's own page and were not checked on the regulators' registers in this review. The page does not describe how client funds are held.

## Trading conditions

The site states the company was founded in 2014 and advertises maximum leverage of 1:500. ECN Standard shows spreads from 0.8 pips with no commission, while ECN Prime and Prime+ show spreads from 0.0 pips with a $3 per lot per side commission on forex and metals. Minimum deposit figures on the homepage conflict ($0 in the headline, $50 to $500 in the comparison table), so confirm the requirement for your account type.

## Platforms

BlackBull supports MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, cTrader and TradingView, plus its own CopyTrader and Invest products.

## Who is it for?

Active traders who want raw pricing and a choice of platforms may find it of interest, provided they accept offshore regulation. It does not accept clients from the USA or Canada. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
