## Regulation and safety

TPFx is operated by PT Trijaya Pratama Futures, Jakarta. BAPPEBTI's broker directory lists the firm with business licence no. 407/BAPPEBTI/SI/VII/2004 (checked 2026-11-13). The company also lists membership of the Jakarta Futures Exchange (JFX), the futures clearing house KBI and the industry association ASPEBTINDO, and says it has more than 20 years of experience in Indonesian futures. It warns about impersonation sites and says it does not accept cash deposits.

## Trading conditions

Published accounts: Standard (variable, $10 minimum, spread from 1.0 pip, $1 commission), Professional (from $2,500, 0.4 pip, $2 commission) and Zero ECN ($5,000, 0 pip, $5 commission), plus fixed-variable and Prime variants. Maximum leverage is 1:400 on all accounts.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 (desktop, web, mobile) and the TPFx mobile app.

## Who is it for?

Indonesian-speaking traders who want a locally licensed futures broker with a low entry deposit. Leverage of 1:400 is high risk.

Trading involves a high risk of loss.
