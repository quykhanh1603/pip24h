## Regulation and safety

GTCFX operates through separate legal entities, and the broker states that a licence held by one does not extend to the others. GTC Global Trade Capital Co. Limited (Vanuatu, company 40354) appears on the VFSC Financial Dealers licensee list as an active Class A, B, C licensee dated 2 June 2023 (checked 2026-11-17). GTC Global Ltd (Mauritius, licence GB22200292) and GTC Global Trading Ltd (Anjouan, licence L16283/GTC) are cited by the broker, but we could not confirm them on the regulators' registers. The about page also lists the FCA, ASIC, CMA and FSCA among regulators without naming licensed entities, so those claims are not relied on here. Vanuatu and Anjouan are offshore jurisdictions.

## Trading conditions

The Standard account has spreads from 1.0 pips, no commission, no minimum deposit and leverage up to 1:2000. The ECN account offers spreads from 0.0 pips with a $5 per lot commission, a $3,000 minimum deposit and leverage up to 1:500.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and the GTC Go app (desktop, web and mobile) are offered. The broker says it has operated since 2012.

## Who is it for?

Traders comfortable with offshore regulation who want low entry requirements. Residents of the United States, Japan and Australia are excluded. CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
