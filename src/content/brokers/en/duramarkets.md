## Regulation and safety

DuraMarkets Ltd is incorporated in the Comoros Union (registered number HT2024018) and states that it is licensed by the Mwali International Services Authority (MISA) on Mohéli island as an International Brokerage and Clearing House, licence number BFX2024033. We could not confirm the entry on a public register, so the licence is listed as unverified, and third-party review sites quote differing licence numbers. MISA is an offshore authority that does not provide the investor protections associated with tier-1 regulators.

## Trading conditions

The website advertises raw spreads from 0.0 pips, leverage up to 500x and instant withdrawals typically processed within an hour. Account types include a Standard account from $10 (spreads from 0.4 pips), a swap-free account from $100 (spreads from 0.6 pips with a $2 commission) and a VIP raw account from $500 (spreads from 0.0 pips with a $2 commission per 100k round-turn). The site also promotes large deposit bonuses, which come with terms and conditions.

## Platforms

DuraMarkets offers MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5, along with VPS hosting, PAMM accounts and an IB programme.

## Who is it for?

DuraMarkets targets cost-conscious traders who want a very low entry deposit and high leverage and who accept the risks of an offshore, recently established broker. Anyone who needs strong regulatory protection or a long operating history should look at brokers licensed by a tier-1 regulator.
