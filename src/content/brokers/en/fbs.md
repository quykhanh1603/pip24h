FBS is a retail forex broker with a strong presence across Asia, known for making it easy and cheap to open a first trading account.

## Trading Fees

The Standard account charges through the spread with no commission, while the ECN-style Zero Spread account offers tighter pricing plus a per-lot commission for cost-conscious traders.

## Should you trade with FBS?

A reasonable starting point for beginners who want a low-cost entry, though the very high available leverage means new traders should size positions carefully rather than using the maximum on offer.
