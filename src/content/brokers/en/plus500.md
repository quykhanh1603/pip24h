## Regulation and safety

Plus500UK Ltd appears in the FCA's investment firms register under reference 509909, and Plus500CY Ltd is on CySEC's register with licence 250/14 (dated 10 October 2014). Plus500's own FAQ also lists ASIC (AFSL 417727), MAS (CMS100648), DFSA, the Seychelles FSA and others, which we did not check on those registers. The parent is listed on the London Stock Exchange. Wikipedia records earlier sanctions: a 2012 FSA fine, a 2015 voluntary restriction agreed with the FCA over anti-money-laundering controls and a 2017 payment to Belgium's FSMA.

## Trading conditions

Plus500 earns mainly through the spread, which is built into its quoted prices. Its FAQ cites a EUR/USD example of 0.6 pips, and details are shown per instrument on the platform. EU forex leverage is capped at 1:30, with lower caps of 1:5 on shares and ETFs. There is no single minimum deposit: it depends on the payment method and region.

## Platforms

Plus500 uses its own platform on web, iOS and Android; it also runs Plus500 Invest for share dealing and futures apps for US clients.

## Who is it for?

It suits traders who prefer a simple proprietary platform and a listed provider. Those wanting MetaTrader, copy trading or very low raw spreads should compare alternatives. CFD trading carries a high risk of loss.
