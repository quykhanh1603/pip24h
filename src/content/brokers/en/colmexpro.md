## Regulation and safety

Colmex Pro Ltd is listed on CySEC's register of Cypriot investment firms under licence 123/10 (issued 2010), which we saw on the regulator's own register on 2026-11-22. The company also states an FSCA (South Africa) financial services provider number, 46990, which we did not check on that register, and says the Israel Securities Authority permits it to offer services in Israel without supervising its activity.

CySEC's decisions page records a €100,000 administrative fine from 2016 and a €200,000 settlement approved in February 2025.

## Trading conditions

The broker concentrates on US-listed stocks, ETFs and options, plus IPOs. According to its site, new clients can no longer open CFD positions as of 30 January 2026. The site advertises real market spreads without mark-up and charges per-share commissions on equity plans. Margin interest is quoted at 0.022% plus the Fed rate per 365 days above 1:1. The FAQ gives a $1,000 minimum for an equity account. Margin equity accounts offer up to 1:20 leverage, and older documents show 1:30 on major forex CFD pairs.

## Platforms

Colmex Pro 2.0 is available on web, desktop and mobile, with TradingView and MetaTrader 4 also supported.

## Who is it for?

It suits active traders of US equities who accept a higher deposit and want a CySEC-supervised broker. Residents of the US, Canada, Japan, Iran, Myanmar and North Korea cannot open accounts. Trading involves risk, and leveraged products can lose more than you expect.
