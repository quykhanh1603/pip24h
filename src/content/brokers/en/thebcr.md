## Regulation and safety

BCR Co Pty Ltd is a British Virgin Islands company (No. 1975046). Its website states it is licensed by the BVI Financial Services Commission under licence number SIBA/L/19/1122 and invites clients to check the public register. We could not confirm the entry on the register, so the licence is shown as unverified. The Kanto Local Finance Bureau of Japan's Ministry of Finance lists BCR Co Pty Ltd in its warnings about firms conducting financial instruments business without Japanese registration (notice dated 2026-01-29), and IOSCO's I-SCAN database carries a related entry. The BCR website also publishes a notice about impersonation of its brand, so clients should confirm they use the official domain.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises 500+ products, spreads from 0.0 pips, up to 500:1 maximum leverage and a $50 minimum deposit. The forex page mentions a $3.00 commission per side and up to 400:1 leverage on forex CFDs. Markets include forex, shares, metals, commodities and index CFDs.

## Platforms

BCR offers MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 with web versions and mobile apps, plus calendars, glossary and education sections.

## Who is it for?

BCR may appeal to traders comfortable with offshore regulation and high leverage. Because of the Japanese regulatory warning and the lack of a tier-1 licence, residents of Japan in particular should be cautious and check local rules before opening an account.
