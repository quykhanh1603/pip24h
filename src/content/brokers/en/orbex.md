## Regulation and safety

Orbex states it has served clients since 2011. Its main regulated entity, Orbex Global Limited, holds a Mauritius Financial Services Commission licence of type SEC-2.1B Investment Dealer; the FSC register of licensees lists the firm with a licence date of 26 July 2018 (checked 2026-11-14). The site also refers to a Seychelles FSA securities dealer licence held by Orbex Limited and an SVG registration for Orbex LLC, but gives no verifiable numbers, so those are shown as unverified. The website's own pages name different entities as operator of orbex.com, so clients should read the terms to see which company they contract with. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction without an investor compensation scheme comparable to the EU or UK.

## Trading conditions

Orbex advertises spreads from 0.0 pips on MT4 and MT5 and leverage up to 1:500. The minimum deposit is not stated on the homepage and varies across third-party sources, so it is omitted.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 are offered with web terminals and a mobile app, plus FIX API access.

## Who is it for?

Orbex may suit experienced traders outside restricted jurisdictions who accept offshore regulation in exchange for high leverage and MetaTrader access. Those who want a top-tier regulator or compensation scheme should consider other options. Trading CFDs carries a high risk of loss.
