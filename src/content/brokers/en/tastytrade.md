## Regulation and safety

tastytrade, Inc. is a US broker-dealer. FINRA BrokerCheck lists it under CRD 277027 (SEC file number 8-69649), formed in Delaware on 22 June 2015 and approved by FINRA in March 2016. Customer accounts are introduced to Apex Clearing Corporation on a fully disclosed basis. The firm describes itself as a member of FINRA, SIPC and NFA; futures accounts are segregated at Apex but are not SIPC-protected.

BrokerCheck also records disciplinary matters: a censure and $850,000 fine in a FINRA settlement dated 21 July 2026 concerning best-execution reviews of equity orders, and a $30,000 fine in November 2024 over inaccurate complaint reporting.

## Trading conditions

Per the website, stocks and ETFs carry $0 commission. Stock and ETF options cost $1.00 per contract to open (capped at $10 per leg) and $0 to close. Futures and futures options cost $1.00 and $1.25 per contract to open and close, and micro futures $0.75. Other fees apply. There is no minimum to open an account, but margin privileges require $2,000 of equity, and standard margin accounts offer 2x leverage. Accounts under $25,000 are subject to pattern day trader rules.

## Platforms

Desktop, web and mobile apps are offered, with a strategy builder, order-chain tracking for multi-leg trades, options backtesting and an API.

## Who is it for?

US-based options and futures traders who want low per-contract costs and analytical tools. Traders looking for MetaTrader-style forex or CFD trading will find a different product here.

Trading involves risk, including loss of capital.
