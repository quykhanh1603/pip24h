## Regulation and safety

ZFX and Traze are approved trade names of Zeal Capital Market (Seychelles) Limited, which the broker says holds Securities Dealer licence SD027 from the Seychelles Financial Services Authority (company number 8422618-1). The FSA's capital markets list names the entity with its ZFX and TRAZE trade names; the list does not show licence numbers. The group also lists a UK company, Zeal Capital Market (UK) Limited, but the website gives no UK regulator or licence number for it, so we do not count it as a licence. Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than the UK, Australia or EU.

## Trading conditions

The homepage lists three accounts: Mini (from $50, minimum FX spread 1.5), Standard (from $200, 1.3) and ECN (from $1,000, 0.2). Maximum leverage is shown as 1:2000 on all three. Wikipedia and several reviews date the ZFX brand to 2017, while the broker's help centre says 2016.

## Platforms

MT4 and MT5 are offered on Windows, Mac and mobile, alongside the ZFX Mobile App, ZFX WebTrader and a MAM (money-manager) service.

## Who is it for?

It may suit experienced traders who accept offshore regulation and want high leverage and a range of account sizes. Very high leverage is unsuitable for most beginners. CFDs carry a high risk of losing money.
