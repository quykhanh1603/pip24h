## Regulation and safety

Tradeview operates through several group entities. Its regulation page lists Tradeview Ltd. (Cayman Islands, CIMA securities investment business licence no. 585163), TVM Global Ltd. (Labuan FSA money broker), iLC Brokers Ltd. (Mauritius FSC) and Tradeview Europe Ltd. (Malta, MFSA Class 2 investment services). We confirmed the Cayman licence on CIMA's published licensee list (checked 2026-11-13) and found iLC Brokers Ltd. on the Mauritius FSC register of licensees. The Labuan and Malta licences were not confirmed on the regulators' registers. The Cayman and Mauritius licences are offshore (Tier 3), so client protection differs from EU or UK regimes. The company states that client funds are held in segregated accounts and that it does not accept US clients.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises spreads from 0.0 pips with a flat commission, leverage up to 1:400 and micro lots. It lists forex, CFDs, stocks, futures, commodities and indices. A minimum deposit was not stated on the pages we reviewed.

## Platforms

Tradeview offers MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and cTrader, plus professional platforms such as Sterling, Takion, DAS, CQG and Multicharts. It says it is unrelated to the TradingView charting service.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders who want raw-spread conditions and institutional-style platforms and accept offshore regulation. Check which entity you are onboarded to before depositing.

Trading CFDs carries a high risk of loss.
