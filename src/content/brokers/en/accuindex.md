AccuIndex (legal entity Accuindex EU Limited) is a forex and CFD broker founded in 2016, headquartered in Ebène, Mauritius, and regulated by both CySEC (Europe) and Mauritius' FSC.

## Trading Costs

AccuIndex offers 4 account types: Standard (from $100, up to 1:100 leverage, spreads from 1.4 pips), Islamic (same as Standard, swap-free), Pro (from $5,000, up to 1:400 leverage, spreads from 0 pips), and Raw (from $25,000, spreads from 0 pips). Note that EU clients are limited to 1:50 maximum leverage under CySEC's MiFID rules.

## Should You Trade With AccuIndex?

AccuIndex suits traders who value regulatory credibility (thanks to its CySEC license) and are comfortable starting with a modest Standard account. That said, reaching the lowest spreads requires substantial capital (Raw/Pro accounts), and it's worth noting WikiFX has logged some user complaints about the withdrawal process — do your own research and manage risk carefully before trading larger amounts.
