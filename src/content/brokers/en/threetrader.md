## Regulation and safety

The broker's footer names ThreeTrader Global Limited, licensed by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (company number 40430). We found this entity listed as an active financial dealer (classes A, B, C) on the VFSC licensee list dated 19 May 2025. A second entity, ThreeTrader Global (MU) Pty Ltd, is shown as registered with the Mauritius FSC (GB24203958); we did not confirm it on the Mauritius register. Both are offshore jurisdictions with lighter investor protection than the UK, Australia or EU. The broker also states that it is a member of The Financial Commission.

## Trading conditions

Two account types are advertised: Raw Zero, with spreads from 0.0 and a commission of 400 yen per lot, and Pure Spread, with spreads from 0.5 and no commission. Maximum leverage is stated as 1:1000 on both. The minimum lot size is 0.01. Servers are located in NY4 and the broker advertises ECN execution.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 across desktop, web and mobile, including automated trading with EAs.

## Who is it for?

The profile suits experienced, cost-focused traders who accept offshore regulation and want raw-spread pricing on MetaTrader. Beginners and anyone who needs a top-tier regulator should consider alternatives. Residents of the US, Iran, North Korea and Myanmar are excluded. CFDs carry a high risk of losing money.
