## Regulation and safety

GBE brokers Ltd (company HE324205, Limassol) states that it is regulated by CySEC under CIF licence 240/14, and that its Hamburg branch is registered with BaFin (BaFin-ID 10148129). GBE's own Pillar 3 disclosure confirms the licence date of 27 June 2014. We could not open the CySEC or BaFin registers in this review, so the licences are marked unverified. The site also advertises investor compensation insurance up to EUR 300,000 via Lloyd's and discloses that 79% of retail accounts lose money.

## Trading conditions

Classic ($1,000), Pro ($10,000) and VIP ($100,000) accounts show DAX spreads of 1.20, 1.10 and 1.00 with commissions on forex. Forex is advertised from 0 pips spread. Leverage ratios are on a separate page we did not read.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and TradingView.

## Who is it for?

Traders, particularly German-speaking, who prefer an EU-regulated CFD broker and can meet a larger initial deposit. CFDs are high risk.
