Tickmill is a broker focused on low-cost ECN trading, popular among scalpers and traders running automated strategies.

## Trading Fees

The Pro account provides spreads from 0.0 pips with a per-lot commission, while the Classic account bundles costs into a slightly wider spread with no commission.

## Should you trade with Tickmill?

Well suited to traders who prioritize execution speed and tight raw spreads and don't need access to cTrader or a wide range of exotic instruments.
