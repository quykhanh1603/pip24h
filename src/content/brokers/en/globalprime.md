## Regulation and safety

Global Prime is a trading name of FMGP Trading Group Pty Ltd (ABN 74 146 086 017), which the broker says holds Australian Financial Services Licence 385620; the company's own disclosure documents list this number and the ABN register shows the entity active since 2010, but we could not open ASIC's register to confirm the licence, so it is marked unverified. The trading entity is Gleneagle Securities Pty Limited, a Vanuatu company (40256) that appears on the VFSC financial dealers licensee list dated 19 May 2025 as an active class C licensee. Which entity serves a client depends on the client's location, and offshore clients typically fall under the Vanuatu licence.

## Trading conditions

The site advertises a $0 minimum account size, spreads from 0.0 pips on majors, and leverage up to 1:1000 (adjustable down to 1:30). It says it has operated for over 16 years.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 are available, on desktop and mobile.

## Who is it for?

It suits experienced traders who want raw-spread pricing and are comfortable with the regulatory split between Australia and Vanuatu. CFDs carry a high risk of losing money.
