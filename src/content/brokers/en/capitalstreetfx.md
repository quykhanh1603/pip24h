## Regulation and safety

The website's footer explains that clients are served by different entities. Capital Street Intermarkets Limited, registered in Mauritius, is described as regulated by the FSC under number C112010690 and manages ECN and prime accounts. Institutional clients, STP accounts and promotions are managed by Capital Street Bancclear Corporation, registered in St. Vincent and the Grenadines under number 22064-IBC-2014. Technology services are provided by Artha Labs in the UAE. We could not confirm these entries on the regulators' registers, so both are shown as unverified, and the SVG number appears to be a company registration rather than a financial licence. The site advises clients to check which entity services their account.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises 2000+ markets across forex, crypto, indices, commodities, stocks and ETFs, spreads from 0 pips, leverage of 1:1000 and above (up to 1:10000 in promotions) and trading on weekends. Account types are Basic, Classic, Professional, Zero and VIP. We did not find a clear minimum deposit. The site also runs bonus promotions of up to 900%.

## Platforms

Three platforms are mentioned, including Alt X and FxyFi Act, plus copy trading and an API for algorithmic trading.

## Who is it for?

Capital Street FX is aimed at experienced traders who accept offshore regulation and very high leverage. Because ultra-high leverage can wipe out an account quickly and tier-1 protections are absent, it is not suitable for beginners or anyone who needs investor-compensation protection.
