## Regulation and safety

Trade.com is a trade name of Lead Capital Global Ltd, incorporated in Mauritius. The Mauritius FSC register of licensees lists Lead Capital Global Limited as an Investment Dealer (Broker) with a licence date of 2 March 2020 (checked 2026-11-12).

The website's footer also shows UK FCA, CySEC and US FINRA/SEC numbers. We could not match these to Lead Capital Global: searching the FCA reference the site shows (738538) returned a different firm. The site also states that it does not serve UK, EU, Swiss or US residents and that no investor compensation system applies. We therefore treat the FSC licence as the only relevant authorisation and do not list the others.

## Trading conditions

The Silver account starts at $100, Gold at $10,000 and the top tier at $100,000+. Spreads are advertised from 0.3 pips. The number of instruments is given as 850+ in one place and 1,200+ in another. Maximum leverage and founding year are not stated on the pages we read.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 5 (including a macOS version), WebTrader and the TRADE mobile app.

## Who is it for?

Traders comfortable with offshore regulation who want a mid-sized CFD range. Check the legal entity and regulation page for your country before opening an account. CFDs carry a high risk of loss.
