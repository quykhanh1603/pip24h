## Regulation and safety

The FXPrimus website names three companies. Primus Global Ltd (Cyprus) holds CySEC licence 261/14, granted on 22 December 2014; this entry was seen on the CySEC investment-firm register on 2026-11-19, where the firm is listed under its former name FX Primus Europe (CY) Ltd with the approved domain fxprimus.eu. Primus Markets INTL Ltd is registered in Vanuatu and states that it is authorised by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (licence 14595); we did not confirm this on the VFSC register. Primus Solutions Ltd may act as a payment agent. The site says client funds are held in segregated bank accounts but does not say which entity holds them or which entity serves which clients, so check the contract entity before opening an account.

## Trading conditions

Account types listed are PrimusCLASSIC, PrimusPRO, PrimusZERO and PrimusSYNTHETICS, plus PAMM and copy trading. The homepage advertises spreads from 0.0 pips on premium accounts and leverage of up to 1:2000 on selected accounts, with lower limits on indices, oil and crypto. Live spreads are shown on the platform. A minimum deposit is not stated on the official pages we reviewed.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and a web trader.

## Who is it for?

FXPrimus suits traders who want MetaTrader access and a range of account types and who accept that the main trading entity is offshore. Those who need EU-level protection should confirm that they are being onboarded under the Cyprus entity. Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of loss.
