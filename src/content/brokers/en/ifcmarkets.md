## Regulation and safety

The website says IFCMARKETS. CORP. is licensed by the British Virgin Islands Financial Services Commission under certificate SIBA/L/14/1073 and displays a Professional Indemnity Insurance badge from Lloyd's of London. No coverage details or investor compensation scheme are given. We could not check the BVI FSC register, so the licence is marked unverified. The company states it has been operating for 20 years and serves clients in 193 countries; it does not accept residents of the United States, Russia or the BVI.

## Trading conditions

Account minimums range from $15 on Nano/Micro accounts to $1,000 on Standard. Maximum leverage is stated as 1:400 with a 10% stop-out level, and spreads start from 0.0 pips on ECN accounts. Instruments include forex, precious metals, indices, stocks, crypto, commodities, futures and ETFs.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on the proprietary NetTradeX platform as well as MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

Small-account traders and those who want a proprietary platform with broad CFD coverage and accept offshore regulation.

Leveraged trading involves a high risk of loss.
