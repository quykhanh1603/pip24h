MEXC is a crypto exchange that has built its reputation on listing a very wide range of altcoins early, alongside competitive spot trading fees.

## Trading Fees

MEXC offers a 0% maker fee on standard spot trading for many pairs, with a small taker fee, and separate fee tiers for futures contracts.

## Should you trade with MEXC?

A good option for traders looking to access newer or smaller altcoins not always listed elsewhere, while sticking to major coins if you prefer to avoid the liquidity risk of very small tokens.
