## Regulation and safety

The client-facing entity for this site is INGOT SC Ltd, which the company says holds Securities Dealer licence SD117 from the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles. On 2026-11-23 we found INGOT SC Ltd (trade names INGOT Brokers, INGOT, Rise) on the FSA's capital markets register; the register page does not show the licence number, which comes from the company's own website. The site also mentions licences from other regulators, which we could not confirm and do not rate. Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction; protections are generally weaker than under FCA or ASIC rules.

## Trading conditions

INGOT advertises zero commission and no deposit or withdrawal fees. Regional account pages show an EVO account from $10 (EUR/USD markup from about 0.7 pips) and a Standard account from $100 (about 1.2 pips); figures vary by regional site. Dynamic leverage of up to 1:5000 is advertised, which can magnify losses very quickly.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, the INGOT Copy Trader and an iOS/Android app are offered.

## Who is it for?

It may suit traders comfortable with an offshore-regulated broker who want a low minimum deposit. Clients from the USA are not accepted, and CFDs carry a high risk of losing money. This is a data profile compiled from public sources, not a hands-on review.
