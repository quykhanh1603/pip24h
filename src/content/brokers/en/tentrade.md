## Regulation and safety

TenTrade is a brand of Evalanch Ltd (Seychelles registration 8429760-1), which states it holds Financial Services Authority licence SD082. The FSA business register lists Evalanch Ltd as a Securities Dealer with the trade names TENTRADE and 10Trade, although the register page we read did not show the licence number or status. The Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than top-tier regulators. The site also names SKAI Consultancy Ltd in Cyprus as an independent representative, which it says is not authorised in Cyprus and carries out no regulated activities.

## Trading conditions

PRO, Cent, Swap Free and Dynamic Leverage accounts have a $0 minimum deposit and spreads from 1.1 pips; the ECN account requires $25 with spreads from 0 pips and a $3 per lot per side commission. Leverage reaches 1:500 on most accounts and 1:5000 on the Dynamic Leverage account. The site also promotes funded-account challenges with evaluation fees from $32.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 5 (desktop, web, mobile) and a web terminal.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders who accept offshore regulation and want a very low entry barrier. CFDs are leveraged and can lose your entire investment.
