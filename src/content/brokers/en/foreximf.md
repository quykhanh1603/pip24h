## Regulation and safety

FOREXimf is the website of PT International Mitra Futures, based in Bandung, Indonesia. Its regulation page lists a BAPPEBTI business licence (No. 736/BAPPEBTI/SI/6/2005), membership of the Jakarta Futures Exchange (JFX), the Indonesia Commodity and Derivatives Exchange (ICDX), and the Indonesian clearing houses KBI and ICH. It also lists an OJK in-principle approval (S-227/PM.02/2025) and a Bank Indonesia permit dated August 2025. The page itself describes the OJK item as an in-principle approval rather than a final licence. These numbers are as stated by the broker; Pip24h has not confirmed them on the official registers.

## Trading conditions

The homepage describes two accounts. The Classic account requires Rp 250,000 and quotes spreads from 2.5 pips. The Gold account requires Rp 5,000,000 and quotes XAUUSD spreads from 1.0 pips. Both list maximum leverage of 1:500. The broker states it has served clients since 2004.

## Platforms

Trading is offered on MetaTrader 4 and the QuickPro mobile app. The site notes that FOREXimf is transitioning to the QuickPro brand.

## Who is it for?

It mainly suits Indonesian residents who want a locally based futures broker with a small starting deposit. Traders should check the current licence status with BAPPEBTI and read the risk disclosure before depositing. Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.
