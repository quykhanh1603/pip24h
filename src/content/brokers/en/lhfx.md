## Regulation and safety

LHFX's homepage states that the website operator is Longhorn Ltd, holding a Mauritius FSC Investment Dealer licence (GB23202204), that the MetaTrader 5 platform operator is LHFX SA (PTY) Ltd under FSCA FSP 52816, and that client trading accounts are opened with LHFX International Ltd. The site does not name a licence for LHFX International Ltd, so it is unclear which regulator covers the client contract. The FSC and FSCA registers could not be retrieved during this research (the Mauritius register blocked access on 2026-11-16), so both licences are marked unverified. Mauritius is an offshore regulator without a compensation scheme.

## Trading conditions

The minimum deposit is $10 in cryptocurrency or $20 by card, and there is no maximum. The standard account shows raw spreads from 0.0 pips with a $3 per side commission per lot ($6 round turn); Islamic accounts pay $3.50 per side with administration fees. Leverage goes up to 1:500, adjustable in the client portal. Scalping, hedging and Expert Advisors are unrestricted. Deposits and withdrawals are free, and the broker reports typical crypto withdrawals in under 20 minutes. 151 markets are offered across forex, crypto, stocks, indices and commodities. On Trustpilot, the site shows 4.4/5 from 127 reviews.

## Platforms

All trading is on MetaTrader 5 (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android) and the LHFX WebTrader.

## Who is it for?

LHFX suits cost-conscious scalpers and EA users who want small minimums and fast crypto funding and accept offshore regulation.
