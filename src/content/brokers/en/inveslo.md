## Regulation and safety

Inveslo Limited is registered in Mauritius under company number C230595. Its website says the company holds an Investment Dealer licence, number GB25205645, issued by the Financial Services Commission (FSC) of Mauritius. We were unable to locate the entry on the FSC's online register, so the licence is listed as unverified. The site also refers to Financial Commission membership and segregated client funds. Mauritius is an offshore jurisdiction without the investor-compensation schemes found in the UK or EU, so clients should weigh that before depositing.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises 300+ instruments across forex, spot metals, energies and indices, with spreads from 0.1 pips and leverage potential of up to 1:2000. The account lineup includes a Standard STP account from $30, a commission-free ECN account from $100, an ECN account from $250 and a Premium account from $20,000 (with leverage up to 1:200). Maximum leverage and spreads depend on the account type and jurisdiction.

## Platforms

Trading is offered on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 on desktop, web and mobile, together with a social/copy-trading feature and standard calculators and market analysis tools.

## Who is it for?

Inveslo targets traders who want a small starting deposit and high leverage and who accept offshore regulation. Traders who prioritise strong regulatory protection, compensation schemes or a long track record may prefer a broker authorised by a tier-1 regulator. Leveraged CFDs carry a high risk of loss.
