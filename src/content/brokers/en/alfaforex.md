## Regulation and safety

The site footer names the operator as OOO Alfa-Forex (Moscow) and states that it holds professional securities-market participant licence No. 045-14070-020000, issued by the Bank of Russia on 20 December 2018 for forex-dealer activity. It also says the company is on the Bank of Russia's forex-dealer register and is a member of the self-regulatory Forex Dealers Association (register no. 009). We could not open the Bank of Russia register itself, so the licence is marked unverified. The company says it was established by Alfa-Bank.

## Trading conditions

The website states a minimum deposit of 3,000 rubles or the equivalent in US dollars or euros. According to the site's FAQ, maximum leverage is 1:40, in line with Russian law. Spreads are described as low, but no figures are published on the pages we read. Products are described as high risk that can lead to loss of the entire deposit.

## Platforms

Trading is offered on MetaTrader 5 for desktop, iOS, Android and in the browser (MT-Web). The site also mentions accounts of an MT4 type.

## Who is it for?

Russian-speaking residents who want a domestically licensed forex dealer with conservative leverage. International traders will find a Russian-language service with limited public cost data.

Trading forex involves a high risk of loss.
