## Regulation and safety

LMAX Global is a trading name of LMAX Broker Limited, authorised by the UK Financial Conduct Authority under firm reference number 783200 (authorised since 15 December 2017 per the FCA Register, which we checked on 2026-11-17). The register shows the firm may hold and control client money. LMAX Group's separate exchange entity, LMAX Limited, has FCA reference 509778 (authorised 2010). LMAX also operates regional entities, including Mauritius and New Zealand, with their own terms.

## Trading conditions

LMAX's FAQ states a minimum initial deposit of $10,000 or the equivalent in the base currency. Published leverage figures differ by entity (for example 200:1 on FX for the Mauritius entity, and lower for UK retail clients under FCA rules), so check the terms of the entity you would use. Specific spreads and commissions were not stated on the pages reviewed.

## Platforms

Clients can trade through the LMAX web platform and through MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

The high minimum deposit and exchange-style market model point to experienced or higher-volume traders rather than beginners. CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
