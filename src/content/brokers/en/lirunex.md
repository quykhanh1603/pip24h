## Regulation and safety

Lirunex operates through several group companies. **Lirunex Ltd** (Cyprus) is listed on the CySEC register of Cypriot investment firms under licence 338/17, dated 25 September 2017, with the trade name LX Prime (checked 2026-11-18). **Lirunex Limited** (Mauritius) states it holds FSC licence GB24203882; this was not found on the FSC register during our check, so it is shown as unverified. A Dubai entity, Lirunex Financial Services L.L.C, holds a UAE CMA Category 5 licence that, according to the company, covers only financial advisory and introduction services, not trading or holding client funds. The website does not say which entity serves which clients, so check the entity named in your client agreement.

## Trading conditions

Four account types are offered: LX-Standard and LX-Standard Cent (spreads from 1.5, no commission), LX-Prime (raw spreads from 0.0 pips, $8 per lot) and LX-Pro (from 0.0 pips, $4 per lot, $10,000 minimum deposit). Standard and Prime have no stated minimum deposit. Maximum leverage is 1:1000 on FX and metals for balances up to $5,000, falling in tiers to 1:100 above $80,000; indices, energy and crypto are capped at 1:200 and stocks at 1:20. Margin call and stop-out levels are 50% and 25%. Swap-free accounts are available on all but the Pro account.

## Platforms

Trading is available on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5, a web terminal and the Lirunex mobile app. PAMM, MAM and copy trading are listed for all account types.

## Who is it for?

Lirunex suits traders who want low entry cost and raw-spread pricing on MetaTrader. High leverage and the split of entities and licences mean traders should read the client agreement carefully. CFDs are complex leveraged products and you can lose your entire investment.
