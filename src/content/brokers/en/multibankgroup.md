## Regulation and safety

MultiBank Group says it is overseen by 18+ regulators through separate entities. We confirmed MEX Europe Ltd on the CySEC register (licence 430/23, dated 22 May 2023) and MEX Global Markets Pte. Ltd. in the MAS Financial Institutions Directory as a Capital Markets Services licensee (checked 2026-11-13). The group also lists MEX Australia Pty Ltd (ASIC AFSL 416279) and MultiBank FX International Corporation (BVI FSC, SIBA/L/14/1068), and licences with BaFin and others; we could not confirm these on registers, and the AFSL number returned another firm in a search, so treat them as unverified. Protections depend on which entity you contract with.

## Trading conditions

Published MT5 accounts: Standard ($50 minimum, spreads from 1.5 pips), Pro ($1,000, from 0.8 pips) and ECN ($10,000, raw spreads from 0.0 pips). The group's own pages cite maximum leverage of both 1:500 and 1:1000, so confirm the figure for your entity and region.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 5 is the main platform, with MetaTrader 4 also offered.

## Who is it for?

Traders who want a large, multi-regulated broker and are located where a regulated entity serves them. Check the entity and leverage before opening an account.

Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
