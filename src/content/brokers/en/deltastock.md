## Regulation and safety

Deltastock AD is based at 6 Korab Planina St, Sofia. It states that it holds a Bulgarian Financial Supervision Commission (FSC) investment-firm licence, RG-03-146, re-issued under MiFID II in 2018, and the company says it has operated since 1998. The FSC's public list of supervised entities names Deltastock AD and its cross-border notifications covering 26 EU states, but the licence number itself did not appear on the pages we read, so we mark it unverified. The firm is also listed on the CNMV (Spain) register of EEA firms providing services cross-border under registration number 2266, dated 8 April 2009 (seen 2026-11-19); this is a passport registration, not a separate Spanish licence. Deltastock says client assets are segregated and retail clients' assets are covered by the Investor Compensation Fund; the compensation limit is not stated on the page we read.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises forex spreads from 0.1 pips and leverage of up to 1:20 for retail clients, in line with EU restrictions. It also shows a risk warning that 59% of retail accounts lose money trading CFDs with the provider. A minimum deposit is not stated on the homepage.

## Platforms

Clients can use the proprietary Delta Trading platform (desktop, web, mobile) or MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

Deltastock suits traders in the EU who prefer a long-established, MiFID-regulated broker with conservative leverage. Traders seeking very high leverage will not find it here. CFDs are complex and carry a high risk of loss.
