## Regulation and safety

Fxview is a brand of the Finvasia Group. The site lists Charlgate Ltd (CySEC licence 367/18, Investor Compensation Fund member), Finvasia Capital South Africa (PTY) Ltd (FSCA licence 50410), FC Global LTD (Comoros-Anjouan licence L16076/FCG, not serving EU, UK or US residents) and a UAE introducer licensed by the CMA. We could not open the regulators' own registers in this review, so the licences are marked unverified. Finance Magnates reports that CySEC reached a EUR 50,000 settlement with Charlgate covering the period from 2020 to mid-2023; this is a settlement, not a warning-list entry.

## Trading conditions

RAW ECN and Islamic accounts start at $50, Premium ECN at $5,000. Spreads start from 0.0 pips with commissions of $1 to $2 per 100k per side on ECN accounts, and the Zero Commission account has spreads from 0.6 pips. Leverage is listed up to 1:1000.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 5, ActTrader and ZuluTrade.

## Who is it for?

Traders wanting ECN pricing and a low entry deposit. The Group's history dates to 2009, while Fxview began serving European CFD clients in 2020. CFDs are high risk.
