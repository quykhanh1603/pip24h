Axi (formerly AxiTrader) is an Australian-regulated broker known for combining a low barrier to entry with genuinely tight ECN pricing on its Pro account tier.

## Trading Fees

The Standard account charges through the spread with no commission, while the Pro account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus a per-lot commission.

## Should you trade with Axi?

A strong choice for traders who value solid regulation and ECN-level pricing without a deposit barrier, as long as MetaTrader 4 covers their platform needs.
