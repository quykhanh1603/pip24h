## Regulation and safety

GatesFX is a brand of GATESFX PTY LTD, incorporated in South Africa in 2014 (registration 2014/148132/07), which states it is an FSCA-authorised financial services provider (FSP 46087). Importantly, the website says clients are onboarded by GATESFX LTD, registered in Saint Lucia (2024-00396), and that this entity does not hold FSP 46087 and is not covered by the FSCA authorisation. We could not check the FSP number on the FSCA's own register during this review, so it is shown as unverified. A JSE verification page lists GATESFX (PTY) LTD as authorised.

## Trading conditions

The ECN Raw Spread account offers spreads from 0.0 pips with a $7 per lot commission and a $500 minimum deposit. The STP Standard account starts from 1.0 pips without commission, also at $500. A bonus account requires $10 and charges $15 per lot commission with spreads from 1.0 pips. Maximum leverage is 1:1000 on the ECN account, 1:500 on the others and 1:200 on stock CFDs.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 5 and TradeLocker are the listed platforms.

## Who is it for?

Traders comfortable with an offshore contracting entity who want low minimums and raw spreads. Those who prioritise onshore protection should note the entity split described above. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of losing money.
