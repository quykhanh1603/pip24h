## Regulation and safety

Interactive Brokers (U.K.) Limited is on the FCA register under reference 208159, and Interactive Brokers Ireland Limited is on the Central Bank of Ireland register under C423427 (authorised 22 December 2020). In the United States, Interactive Brokers LLC is regulated by the SEC and CFTC and is a FINRA and SIPC member; we did not check those registers. The parent company has been listed on Nasdaq (IBKR) since 2007. Wikipedia records a number of regulatory penalties over the years, including FINRA, FCA, CFTC and OFAC matters.

## Trading conditions

Individual, joint and trust accounts have no minimum deposit on IBKR Pro or IBKR Lite. IBKR's pages quote spot forex commissions in basis points of trade size, for example 0.08 to 0.20 basis points on its fact sheet. We did not find a leverage table, so check the margin rules for your region.

## Platforms

The main platform is Trader Workstation, supported by mobile apps and the IBKR Desktop platform. The broker gives access to stocks, options, futures, forex and, in some regions, crypto.

## Who is it for?

Interactive Brokers suits active and experienced investors who want global market access and commission-based pricing. Beginners may find the platform complex. Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.
