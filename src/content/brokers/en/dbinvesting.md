## Regulation and safety

DB Investing is the trade name of DB Invest Limited, which the Seychelles Financial Services Authority lists on its Capital Markets register under the trade name DB Investing. The broker states licence number SD053 as a securities dealer; the register page we read did not show the number itself. A sister entity, DB Invest (MY) Limited, is described by the broker as an investment dealer regulated by the Mauritius FSC (licence GB25204845), which we could not confirm on the register. A UAE entity, DBInvest Financial Services LLC, is described as a Category 5 licensee limited to introduction and promotion, so it does not provide brokerage or execute client trades. The group's holding company is registered in the Abu Dhabi Global Market. Seychelles and Mauritius are offshore jurisdictions with lighter investor protection than the UK, Australia or the EU.

## Trading conditions

The broker's account page lists three account types, all on MetaTrader 5:

- **Standard:** $10 minimum deposit, spreads from 1.0 pip, no commission, leverage up to 1:2000.
- **Raw:** $10 minimum deposit, spreads from 0.0 pips, $3 per lot per side, leverage up to 1:1000.
- **Pro:** $5,000 minimum deposit, spreads from 0.0 pips, $1.5 per lot per side, leverage up to 1:1000.

Swap-free versions are available. Other pages of the site quote different maximum leverage figures, so confirm the terms that apply to your entity before trading. High leverage greatly increases the risk of loss.

## Platforms

Trading runs on MetaTrader 5, with mobile apps and a social trading service offered alongside it.

## Who is it for?

The low entry deposit suits traders who want to test a live account with a small amount and who accept offshore regulation. Traders who prioritise strong regulatory protection should consider brokers holding a tier-1 licence. Trading CFDs and forex involves a high risk of losing money.
