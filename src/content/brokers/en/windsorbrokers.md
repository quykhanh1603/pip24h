## Regulation and safety

Windsor Brokers lists four entities: Windsor Brokers International Ltd (Seychelles FSA), Windsor Markets (Kenya) Limited (Capital Markets Authority, Kenya), Windsor Global Markets Ltd (BVI FSC) and Seldon Investments Limited (Jordan Securities Commission, company no. 1265). The Seychelles FSA capital-markets register lists Windsor Brokers International Ltd (checked 2026-11-13), although the page we saw did not show a licence number and names a related "Windsor Brokers (SC) Limited" dealer. The Kenya, BVI and Jordan licences were not confirmed on regulator registers. The company states that it does not serve residents of the USA, UK or Europe and that 91.40% of retail accounts lose money.

## Trading conditions

The Prime account starts at $50 with spreads from 1.0 pips and no commission. The Zero account offers spreads from 0.0 pips with a commission of about $7-8 per lot round turn (the page showed inconsistent figures, including a $1,000 minimum). VIP Zero has lower commissions on request. Maximum dynamic leverage is 1:2000, reduced to 1:400 for Kenyan clients and 1:100 for forex at the Jordan entity.

## Platforms

Clients trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and a mobile app. Crypto CFDs are unavailable to Jordanian and Kenyan entity clients.

## Who is it for?

Traders who value a long-established brand and low entry deposits, and who accept offshore regulation. Confirm which entity you will contract with.

Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
