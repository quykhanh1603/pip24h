OneRoyal is a forex and CFD broker operating since 2006, headquartered in Limassol, Cyprus with offices in Australia, Vanuatu, and several other countries, regulated by both CySEC (Europe) and ASIC (Australia) — two of the world's most respected financial regulators.

## Trading Costs

OneRoyal offers Cent (from $10), Classic and ECN (from $50, ECN spreads from 0.0 pips), and VIP/ECN Elite (from $10,000) accounts. The headline 1:1000 leverage is only available through the offshore (VFSC Vanuatu) entity; accounts under CySEC/ASIC oversight carry lower leverage caps per regulation.

## Should You Trade With OneRoyal?

OneRoyal suits traders who value a long regulatory track record and want to start small with the Cent account. That said, WikiFX has logged some reports of slow withdrawals and unclear fees — review the withdrawal terms of your specific account carefully before trading larger amounts.
