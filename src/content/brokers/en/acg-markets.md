## Regulation and safety

ACG Markets Ltd (company number 8434915-1) states that it is regulated by the Financial Services Authority (FSA) of Seychelles under Securities Dealer licence SD182. The firm appears on the FSA's capital markets list of regulated entities (checked 2026-11-26); that list shows the name, trade name and address but not the licence number, so SD182 comes from the broker's own disclaimer. Seychelles is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor-protection rules than the UK, Australia or the EU, so clients should read the client agreement carefully. A separate company, ACG Payments Ltd, acts as payment agent for card deposits.

## Trading conditions

The broker publishes two account types, Standard and Raw. Both require a $100 minimum deposit, allow leverage up to 1:500 and trade from 0.01 lots, with servers in London. Standard spreads are typically from 0.7 pips with no commission; Raw spreads start from 0.0 pips with a commission per trade (the amount is not given on the page). Instruments are forex (58 pairs), metals, indices and commodities as CFDs.

## Platforms

Clients trade on MetaTrader 5 or the Alpha Trader platform.

## Who is it for?

ACG Markets may suit traders comfortable with an offshore-regulated broker who want raw spreads and a modest entry deposit. Those who prioritise top-tier regulation or a long track record may prefer a broker with FCA, ASIC or similar oversight. CFDs carry a high risk of rapid loss, and losses can exceed deposits.
