## Regulation and safety

iFX Brokers is the trading name of iFX Brokers Holdings (Pty) Ltd (registration 2017/027249/07), which states that it is an authorised financial services provider with the South African FSCA (FSP 48021) and an OTC derivative provider. The FSCA has also published a warning about people impersonating iFX Brokers under that FSP number, which suggests the number belongs to the firm, but we could not look it up on the FSCA register, so the licence is shown as unverified.

## Trading conditions

Minimum deposits are $10 for the Standard, Islamic and Cent accounts, $250 for Premium and Raw, and $1,000 for VIP. The site elsewhere says no minimum initial deposit is required, so check before funding. Maximum leverage is up to 1:500 on most accounts and 1:1000 on Cent. Spread examples on the site are indicative only and units are not given, so we do not quote a headline spread.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 are offered; Cent and Raw accounts are MT5 only.

## Who is it for?

South African and international traders wanting small-deposit accounts with an FSCA-linked entity. Trading CFDs involves a high risk of losing money.
