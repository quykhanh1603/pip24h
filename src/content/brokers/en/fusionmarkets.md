## Regulation and safety

Fusion Markets is a trading name used by several entities. FMGP Trading Group Pty Ltd (ABN 74 146 086 017) holds Australian Financial Services Licence 385620; the ASIC professional register lists it as current, and the licence covers foreign exchange contracts and derivatives. According to Fusion's regulation page, this entity serves Australian clients only. Fusion Markets International Ltd states it is a securities dealer licensed by the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles (SD096), and Gleneagle Securities Pty Limited states it is a Vanuatu company (number 40256) regulated by the VFSC. We could not confirm those two on the regulators' registers, so they are marked unverified. The broker says client money is held in segregated trust accounts.

## Trading conditions

The website offers Zero and Classic accounts, plus swap-free and a premium programme. It advertises average spreads of 0.0 on forex majors with a $2.25 per side commission, and says there is no minimum account size and no deposit fees. Maximum leverage varies by entity and is not published on the pages we read; Australian retail clients are subject to ASIC's leverage limits.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, cTrader and TradingView. Fusion+ copy trading, a multi-account manager and DupliTrade are also listed.

## Who is it for?

Cost-focused traders who want raw pricing and a choice of platforms. Non-Australian clients should note they will usually be onboarded to an offshore entity and weigh the reduced protection accordingly.

Trading CFDs involves a high risk of loss.
