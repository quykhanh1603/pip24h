## Regulation and safety

Taurex lists three entities. Taurex Global Limited (Seychelles, FSA licence SD092) is described as the issuer and seller of the products on the site. Taurex Limited, an English company (number 11077380), holds FCA firm reference 816055; an FCA warning page about a clone website describes Taurex Limited as the genuine, authorised firm (checked 2026-11-20). The site states it is not directed at UK residents, so the UK authorisation should not be assumed to apply to clients of the Seychelles entity. A third entity, Taurex Financial Services L.L.C. in the UAE, is listed under CMA licence 20200000224 (Category 5, arrangement and advice) and is not authorised to hold client money. The Seychelles FSA register lists Zenfinex Global Limited as a Securities Dealer with the trade names Taurex and Taurex Prime, but the page we read did not show the licence number.

Taurex rebranded from Zenfinex, which was launched in 2020 according to Finance Magnates.

## Trading conditions

The site advertises raw spreads from 0.0 pips on selected instruments, more than 1,500 CFDs and leverage up to 1:2000. A minimum deposit is not stated on the pages reviewed.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, the Taurex Trading App, a FIX API, PAMM and copy trading are listed.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders comfortable with an offshore-issued product and high leverage. CFDs are complex and carry a high risk of rapid losses.
