## Regulation and safety

Ultima Markets lists Ultima Markets UK Limited (FCA, FRN 470325), Ultima Markets (Mauritius) Ltd (FSC Mauritius, GB23201593) and Ultima Markets (Pty) Ltd (FSCA, FSP 52497, intermediary only). The FCA firm checker shows the UK entity as authorised (checked 2026-11-03). However, the broker states that this website is operated by international entities that are not FCA-regulated, so FCA protections such as the FSCS and the Financial Ombudsman do not apply to its clients. It says UK onboarding is expected in 2026. The Mauritius and South African licences were not checked on their registers in this run. The broker is a member of The Financial Commission and cites client insurance through WTW.

## Trading conditions

The site says it has operated since 2016. Per its account page, all account types need a $50 minimum deposit (the Ultimate account requires a $1,000 cumulative deposit to activate). The Standard account has a 1.0-pip minimum spread with no commission; the ECN account has 0.0 pips plus a $5 commission. Leverage is advertised up to 1:2000.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, WebTrader Pro and the Ultima Markets mobile app.

## Who is it for?

Experienced traders outside the UK, US, Singapore and Hong Kong who accept offshore regulation in exchange for high leverage and low entry cost. Very high leverage magnifies losses.

CFDs are high risk and losses can exceed your initial investment.
