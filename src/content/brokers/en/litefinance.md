LiteFinance, previously known as LiteForex, is an offshore-regulated broker offering both forex and crypto CFDs with a low-cost ECN-style account.

## Trading Fees

The ECN account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus commission, while the Classic account bundles costs into a wider spread with no separate commission.

## Should you trade with LiteFinance?

Reasonable for traders comfortable with offshore-only regulation who want low-cost ECN pricing and don't need extensive in-house educational content.
