## Regulation and safety

Finex's website states that PT Finex Bisnis Solusi Futures is an official futures broker in Indonesia under Bappebti licence 47/BAPPEBTI/SI/04/2013. It also lists Bank Indonesia, OJK, Jakarta Futures Exchange (SPAB-161/JFX/02/2013) and Kliring Berjangka Indonesia (03/AK-KBI/PN/II/2022) registrations. The site links to the broker's Bappebti record (bappebti.go.id/pialang_berjangka/detail/133), but that register was not matched in this review, so the licence is shown as unverified.

## Trading conditions

The site says the company was established in 2012 (another section says 2013). The minimum deposit is $10 and spreads are floating from 0.5 pips. Maximum leverage is 1:500 for forex and metals, 1:200 for indices and energy, and 1:25 for stocks.

## Platforms

Trading is on MetaTrader 5 (Windows, Android, iOS and browser) and the Finex Trading app for Android.

## Who is it for?

It mainly suits traders in Indonesia who want a locally registered broker with a low entry deposit. Trading leveraged products involves a high risk of loss.
