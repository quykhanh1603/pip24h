## Regulation and safety

ThinkMarkets operates through several entities. The FCA firm checker shows TF Global Markets (UK) Limited (FRN 629628) as authorised, since 23 January 2015 (checked 2026-11-03). The broker's pages also name TF Global Markets (Aust) Limited (ASIC), CySEC and FSCA licensed entities, and TF Global Markets Int Limited (Seychelles FSA, SD060). Those were not confirmed on their registers in this run, and the AFSL number is given inconsistently across its pages, so we do not quote it. Which entity holds your account depends on your country.

## Trading conditions

The broker traces its origins to ThinkForex in 2010. The Standard account advertises spreads from 0.4 pips with no commission, while ThinkZero offers raw spreads from 0.0 plus commission. Minimum deposits are shown differently across ThinkMarkets pages (between $0 and $500 depending on account and region), so check your regional entity. Maximum leverage is region-dependent, for example 500:1 on the global Standard page versus 30:1 for Australia.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and the proprietary ThinkTrader platform.

## Who is it for?

Traders who want a multi-regulated broker with both commission-free and raw-spread accounts. Confirm the entity and its protections before depositing.

CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
