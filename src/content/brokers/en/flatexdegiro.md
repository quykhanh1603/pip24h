## Regulation and safety

flatexDEGIRO SE is the listed holding company of a pan-European online broker. Its banking entity, flatexDEGIRO Bank SE, appears in BaFin's company database (BaFin-ID 10119766) as a credit institution, and we confirmed the entry on the regulator's own database on 2026-11-22. DEGIRO describes itself as the Dutch branch of the bank and states that deposits are protected up to €100,000.

BaFin has published fines against group entities: €560,000 against flatexDEGIRO Bank AG in December 2025 for how free investment services were advertised, and €1,000,000 against flatexDEGIRO SE in April 2026 for delayed disclosure of inside information.

## Trading conditions

The group says it has executed trades since going into business in 2006 and gives clients access to more than 50 exchanges plus OTC direct trading. On DEGIRO's UK site the first deposit to validate an account can be as low as £0.01, deposits and withdrawals are free, there is no monthly fee, and a €1 handling fee applies to most exchange orders. Currency conversion costs 0.25%. This is an investing-style fee structure, so spreads and leverage figures as used for forex brokers do not apply.

## Platforms

Three brokerage platforms are offered: flatex, DEGIRO, and ViTrade, which is aimed at very active traders.

## Who is it for?

It suits long-term investors in Europe who want low-cost access to shares and ETFs through a bank-backed broker. It is not designed for leveraged forex trading. Investing involves risk, and you can lose part or all of your capital.
