## Regulation and safety

Varchev Finance EOOD is listed as an investment firm on the register of the Bulgarian Financial Supervision Commission (FSC), the national MiFID regulator. The broker's site cites investment intermediary licence No. РГ-03-02-05 (dated 15 March 2006); the FSC list page we read shows the firm, its Varna address and its websites but not the licence number. A separate FSC notice page, filed under the name Varchev Finans EOOD, shows notifications to provide services in Italy and Portugal. The FSC has also warned that an unrelated entity called Vranchev Finance is not connected with the licensed firm, so check the exact legal name before depositing. The firm operates under the EU MiFID framework and ESMA rules.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises EUR/USD spreads from 0.4 pips and gold from 0.42 USD, with no commission on MetaTrader 5 depending on instrument and account type. For non-professional clients, leverage is up to 1:30 on major forex pairs and 1:20 on commodities. The site does not state a minimum deposit on its homepage, so confirm it when opening an account. The site displays a risk warning that 63.41% of non-professional client accounts lose money trading CFDs with the firm.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on MetaTrader 5 or on Varchev Trader, the firm's own platform for desktop, web and mobile.

## Who is it for?

Varchev suits traders in the EU who want a locally supervised broker with a native-language service and capped leverage. Traders seeking very high leverage will not find it under retail terms. CFD trading carries a high risk of losing money.
