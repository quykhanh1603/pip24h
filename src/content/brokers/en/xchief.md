XChief (formerly ForexChief) is a forex and CFD broker founded in 2014, headquartered in Port Vila, Vanuatu with offices in Singapore and Nigeria, operating under a MISA (Comoros) license and registered with South Africa's FSCA.

## Trading Costs

XChief offers 4 account types with different cost structures: Cent (no minimum deposit, spreads from 0.9 pips), Classic+ (from $10, spreads from 0.6 pips), DirectFX (from $50, spreads from 0.3 pips plus commission) and xPRIME (from $2,000, spreads from 0 pips, low commission) — suited to both beginners and professional traders who need ultra-tight spreads.

## Should You Trade With XChief?

XChief suits traders who want to start small thanks to the free-to-open Cent account, or experienced traders who want to take advantage of high leverage and tight spreads on the xPRIME account. That said, keep in mind the broker only holds a lower-tier MISA license (Tier-3, no investor compensation fund), so trade with careful risk management and avoid depositing more than you can afford to lose.
