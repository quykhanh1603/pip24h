## Regulation and safety

Land Prime Ltd is shown on the Mauritius Financial Services Commission's online public register as a SEC-2.1B investment dealer (full service dealer excluding underwriting) with a licence date of 31 December 2024, which we saw on 2026-11-22. The site quotes licence number GB24203734, which we could not match on the register. The site also names Land Prime (SVG), an International Business Company in St. Vincent & the Grenadines, for which no regulator is given.

## Trading conditions

The homepage advertises spreads from 0.0 pips on major pairs such as EURUSD and XAUUSD in its quote table, leverage of up to 2000:1 and negative balance protection. Accounts are Standard, ECN and Cent. Forex, commodities, indices, crypto and stocks are offered. The minimum deposit and commission levels were not stated on the page, so check them before funding an account. Services are not offered to residents of the USA, Cuba, Sudan, Syria or North Korea.

## Platforms

Trading is on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Who is it for?

It may suit experienced traders who want high leverage and accept offshore regulation. Beginners should be cautious: very high leverage greatly increases the chance of losing your deposit. Trading CFDs involves a substantial risk of loss.
