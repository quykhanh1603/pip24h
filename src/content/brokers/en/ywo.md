## Regulation and safety

YWO's Licenses & Regulations page names three entities: YWO (MU) Ltd, licensed by the Mauritius Financial Services Commission under GB25205550; YWO (PTY) Ltd, an authorised financial services provider with South Africa's FSCA under FSP 54357; and YWO (CM) Ltd, licensed by the Mwali International Services Authority of the Comoros under BFX2025026. Third-party sources corroborate the numbers and a press release reports the Mauritius licence was granted in early 2026. Pip24h could not open the FSCA, FSC or MISA registers during this research (the FSC site blocked access on 2026-11-16), so all three licences are marked unverified. None of the entities serves residents of the EU, UK, US, Canada or Australia, and the South African entity excludes South African residents.

## Trading conditions

Three account types are offered. Cent offers micro lots, spreads from 0.6 pips and leverage up to 1:200. Standard has no commission, spreads from 0.6 pips on majors and leverage up to 1:1000. Zero Spread offers spreads from 0.0 pips with a $3.50 per lot per side commission ($7 round turn) and leverage up to 1:1000. Deposits and withdrawals carry no fees, and swap-free trading is available. Instruments include forex, metals, energies, indices, stocks and crypto CFDs.

## Platforms

Trading is on MetaTrader 5. The broker also runs introducing-broker and revenue-share partner programmes.

## Who is it for?

YWO fits active traders in permitted regions who want raw-spread pricing and are comfortable with a young broker whose licences have yet to be independently verified.
