## Regulation and safety

Trade Nation trades through several entities. Its footer lists Trade Nation Financial UK Ltd (FCA, firm reference 525164), Trade Nation Australia Pty Ltd (ASIC, AFSL 422661), Trade Nation Financial (Pty) Ltd (FSCA, licence 49846), Trade Nation Europe Empresa de Investimento, S.A. (CMVM, licence 601), plus Trade Nation Financial Markets Ltd (Seychelles FSA, SD150) and Trade Nation Ltd. (Bahamas SCB, SIA-F216). The FCA firm checker shows the UK entity as authorised (checked 2026-11-03). The other licences were taken from the broker's own disclosures and were not checked on their registers in this run.

The FCA has published a warning about clone websites using this firm's details, so confirm you are on the genuine domain.

## Trading conditions

The broker advertises fixed spreads, including during volatile markets, with variable spreads on commodities. Its EU About page states leverage from 30:1 down to 2:1 depending on the asset. We did not find a minimum deposit or a headline spread figure on the official pages we read.

## Platforms

Clients can trade on Trade Nation's own web and mobile platform, and the broker offers a TradingView integration.

## Who is it for?

Traders who prefer predictable fixed spreads and a client entity under a major regulator such as the FCA or ASIC. Check which entity you would be onboarded to, since protections differ by entity.

Trading CFDs carries a high level of risk to your capital.
