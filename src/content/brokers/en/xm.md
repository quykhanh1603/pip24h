XM is one of the most widely recognized retail forex brokers, known for its low barrier to entry and heavy investment in trader education.

## Trading Fees

The Standard and Micro accounts charge through the spread with no commission, while the Zero account offers tighter spreads plus a per-lot commission for cost-sensitive traders.

## Should you trade with XM?

A solid starting point for beginners thanks to the low minimum deposit and educational resources, though experienced scalpers may prefer a dedicated ECN broker's tighter raw spreads.
