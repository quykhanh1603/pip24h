## Regulation and safety

Titan FX Limited (company number 40313) appears on the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission's Financial Dealers Licensee List with an active status (licence date 29 June 2022, checked 2026-11-02). Vanuatu is an offshore jurisdiction with lighter investor protection than top-tier regulators. The broker also names Atlantic Markets Limited (BVI FSC licence SIBA/L/23/1124) and Titan Markets (Mauritius FSC licence GB20026097); we did not verify these two on their registers. Titan FX states that client funds are held in segregated accounts, that it carries professional indemnity insurance, and that it belongs to the Financial Commission dispute-resolution scheme. The site says it is not supervised by the JFSA and is not aimed at residents of Japan.

## Trading conditions

The broker says it was created in 2014 and operates a no-dealing-desk model with a New York liquidity centre. Account types are Zero Standard, Zero Blade, Zero Micro and a demo account. Spreads are advertised from 0.0. The homepage cites maximum leverage of 1000:1 in one place and 2000:1 in another, so check the current terms for your entity before trading. No minimum deposit figure was stated on the pages we read. Instruments include forex, metals, commodities, index and stock CFDs and cryptocurrencies.

## Platforms

Traders can use MetaTrader 4 and 5, the Titan FX Social copy-trading service, a web terminal and mobile apps.

## Who is it for?

Titan FX may suit experienced traders who want tight raw-style spreads and accept the risks of an offshore licence. Those who prioritise strong regulatory protection should consider brokers supervised by top-tier regulators. CFDs are high-risk products and most retail accounts lose money.
