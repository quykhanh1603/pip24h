## Regulation and safety

MIFX is the trading brand of PT Monex Investindo Futures. BAPPEBTI, the Indonesian commodity futures regulator, lists the firm among its futures brokers with business licence 178/BAPPEBTI/SI/I/2003 (checked 2026-11-03). The listing page does not show a current-status field, so confirm status with BAPPEBTI's legality check. MIFX also states it holds an OJK reference and is a member of the Jakarta Futures Exchange and ICDX, and a clearing member of KBI and ICH; we did not verify those. The company has publicly reported fake sites imitating MIFX, so confirm you are on mifx.com.

## Trading conditions

MIFX says it has operated since 2000. Its support centre gives an entry deposit of Rp500,000 for Ultra Low and Standard Live accounts. Default leverage is 1:100, and the High Leverage option raises it to 1:500 for equity under $50,000, stepping down to 1:200 and 1:100 as equity grows. Spread information is inconsistent across pages, so check the live terms.

## Platforms

MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 (Multilateral accounts run on MT5 only).

## Who is it for?

Indonesian residents who want a locally licensed futures broker rather than an offshore entity.

Trading involves risk and you may lose your capital.
