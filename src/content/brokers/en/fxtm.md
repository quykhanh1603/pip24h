FXTM (ForexTime) is a multi-regulated broker with particularly strong brand recognition across Africa and Asia, offering both simple fixed-spread accounts and ECN-style pricing.

## Trading Fees

The ECN account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus a per-lot commission, while the Standard account bundles costs into a wider spread with no separate commission.

## Should you trade with FXTM?

A solid option for traders who want ECN-level pricing from a well-regulated broker with a low minimum deposit, especially those interested in copy trading via FXTM Invest.
