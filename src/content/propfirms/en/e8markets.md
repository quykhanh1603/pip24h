E8 Markets is a proprietary trading firm that sells one-phase evaluations under the "SimFi™" label: you pay a fee, trade a simulated account against a set of rules, and receive a share of the simulated profit once you pass. Every product it currently offers is single-phase, including the futures-only E8 Zero.

**Important:** E8 accounts are simulated. The firm does not accept deposits for investment and its own terms describe payments as access to educational software. The money you actually risk is the evaluation fee.

## The products

Four programs are on sale. **E8 One** is the most customisable: Classic Markets (forex, commodities, indices, energies) or Perpetual Futures, no payout caps, a daily drawdown that is a hard breach and a Dynamic Drawdown. **E8 Pro** trades the dynamic drawdown for a static one and adds daily payouts with no best-day rule — the requirement is a 1% profit and a 2% daily profit cap. **E8 Signature** is the cheaper, more structured route with payout caps and a "Daily Pause" soft breach. **E8 Zero** is futures-only, with no daily limits, an end-of-day dynamic drawdown, payout caps and a maximum of five payouts per account.

## Rules and drawdowns

On E8 One the numbers are published on the account configurator: a 9% profit target, a 4% daily loss cap and a 6% Dynamic Drawdown on a $100K account. The Dynamic Drawdown is calculated from your highest *closed* balance — close $2,000 of profit and the loss level rises by $2,000, but it stops rising once it reaches your starting balance. E8 calls this "dynamic"; in the table above it is mapped to trailing because that is the closest of the two standard labels. Inside the Challenge there is no consistency rule, but the Performance account carries a 40% best-day rule. News trading is allowed during the Challenge and prohibited on the Performance account, where opening or closing trades, stop-losses and take-profits are all barred around high-impact releases.

## Fees and payouts

E8 One sizes run from $5K to $500K. The $5K account lists at $48, the $100K at $488 and the $500K at $1,998; at the time of checking, a 30% discount was applied sitewide, bringing those to $34, $342 and $1,399. There is no activation fee and the fee is not refunded when you pass. Splits are 80% or 100% depending on the product, and payouts are requested on demand — E8 advertises a first payout from as little as three days.

## Platforms and legal structure

The platform list is split by entity. E8 Funding LLC operates TradeLocker, cTrader and Match-Trader, while E8 Markets Ltd — incorporated in Saint Lucia under registration number 2025-00347, at The Sotheby Building, Rodney Bay — operates MetaTrader 5. Classic-market leverage is 1:30 on forex, 1:15 on indices and metals, and 1:1 on crypto. The firm restricts residents of Cuba, Syria, Iran, Lebanon, Iraq, Yemen, North Korea, Russia, Ukraine and Belarus.

## Is E8 Markets right for you?

E8 is one of the few firms offering accounts at $400K and $500K with no payout caps, and E8 Pro's static drawdown plus daily payouts is a genuinely different product from the usual trailing model. The trade-offs are the unregulated Saint Lucia entity, non-refundable fees and a rule set that changes between the Challenge and the Performance account — read which product you are buying before you pay.
