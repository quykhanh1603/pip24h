FundedNext is a UAE-registered proprietary trading firm founded in 2022. It grew quickly by attacking the two things traders complain about most at older firms: the price of entry and the wait between payouts.

**Important:** FundedNext Accounts use *simulated* capital. Rewards are calculated from your simulated results, and your real financial exposure is the evaluation fee.

## How the evaluation works

There are two main CFD routes. **Stellar 2-Step** asks for 8% in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2, with a 5% daily loss limit, a 10% static maximum loss and trading on at least 5 separate days in each phase. **Stellar 1-Step** compresses this into a single 10% target but tightens the risk: 3% daily loss, 6% maximum loss and a 2-day minimum. Neither route has a time limit, so a slow, low-risk pass is a legitimate strategy.

## Costs and refunds

A $6K Stellar 2-Step account starts at $29.99, and the ladder runs $15K/$109.99, $25K/$189.99, $50K/$269.99, $100K/$529.99 and $200K/$1,049.99. The 2-Step fee is refundable — but note the change made in January 2026: accounts bought from the 12th get the refund with their third payout rather than their first. The 1-Step model pays a passing reward instead. If you choose cTrader or Match-Trader, a $25 platform fee is added and never refunded.

## Rewards and scaling

This is FundedNext's strongest card. Stellar 1-Step pays every 5 business days; Stellar 2-Step pays 21 days after your first trade and then every 14 days. Splits start at 80%, reach 90% through Scale-Up and can go to 95% with paid add-ons. Scale-Up reviews your account after four consecutive months in which your withdrawals grew at least 10%, and raises the account size by 40% each time, up to a ceiling of $4M. Total simulated capital held at once is capped at $300K across your Stellar accounts before scaling.

## Platforms and trading rules

MT4, MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader and TradingView are all supported. Weekend holding is allowed at every stage. News trading is unrestricted during the evaluation, but the funded account applies a News Profit Split Rule: trades opened or closed within 5 minutes either side of a listed high-impact release only contribute 40% of their profit. Expert Advisors are allowed below $50K and banned at $50K and above, which is an unusual restriction and the single most important rule to check before you buy.

## Is FundedNext right for you?

It suits discretionary traders who want money moving back to them quickly and who trade smaller accounts. If you rely on an EA, cap yourself at a $25K account or look elsewhere — and if your edge is news scalping, the 40% rule will eat most of it.
